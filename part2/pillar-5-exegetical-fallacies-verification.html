01 · EVIDENCE AND INTERPRETATION 01 · 證據與詮釋

A claim is not yet an argument 主張尚不等於論證

An exegetical conclusion becomes assessable when its evidentiary route is visible. Begin with a question narrow enough to answer: what does a particular expression contribute to a particular clause, or how does a passage relate to its neighboring units? Identify the textual form under discussion, the relevant context, and the inference connecting observation to conclusion. A statement such as “the word means X” often conceals several distinct claims: that a form has been identified correctly, that X is an available meaning in the period and genre, and that the surrounding construction favors X here. Each can be challenged separately.0102

The purpose of an audit is to make interpretation more responsible, not to make every disputed proposition disappear. A strong analysis records the best alternative, specifies which observation would favor it, and assigns a degree of confidence proportionate to the evidence. An uncertain historical setting should not be converted into a certain theological premise. Conversely, the absence of certainty does not require treating every imaginable reading as equally plausible. The question is whether competing explanations account for the data with comparable scope and fewer unsupported assumptions.03

Even the question posed to a passage can import an answer. A forced choice between two options is useful only when the options are genuinely exclusive and cover the plausible possibilities. An essay title may ask whether a writer is “legalist or libertine,” but the categories require definition before a researcher can determine whether either applies. The writer may address different problems in different parts of an argument, resist the assumptions behind both categories, or pursue another issue altogether. Carson discusses question framing as a logical problem: a question can assume the very point that needs to be demonstrated, or offer a false dichotomy.04

An auditable research question separates description from evaluation. “What contrast does the author draw in this paragraph?” invites inspection of textual relationships; “Why does the author reject all law here?” already assumes a scope that may not be established. The second question can be repaired by first examining which law, practice, audience, or claim is under discussion, then asking whether rejection, qualification, or another relation best describes the passage. A well-framed question also specifies what would count against the favoured conclusion. If no conceivable observation could alter the answer, the inquiry may be a restatement of the interpreter's position rather than a test of it.05

From evidence to conclusion

A persuasive claim also needs a warrant: the reason this kind of observation should support this kind of conclusion. Suppose an interpreter observes that a term appears at the beginning and end of a paragraph and proposes that the repetition frames the unit. The repeated forms can be checked, but the framing claim is stronger than a count. It requires attention to where the unit begins and ends, how the repeated expression functions at both locations, and whether the proposed boundary also explains changes in participants, topic, or argument. If the expression is common throughout a book, its presence alone may provide weaker evidence for a deliberate frame than an unusual recurrence at a decisive transition. A sound argument explains the connection without making rarity an infallible rule.06

The method is not a mechanical scoring system. A negative result on one feature does not automatically veto a reading if other evidence is strong; nor does a list of several weak parallels automatically establish the conclusion. Distinguish independent observations from repeated descriptions of the same observation. Three sentences that all rest on one lexical coincidence do not become three independent witnesses merely because they use different terminology. Conversely, syntactic, literary, and contextual observations may offer complementary support when they address distinct aspects of the claim. State the nature of that support so that a critic can disagree at the appropriate point.07

一項釋經結論只有在證據與推論的路徑清楚可見時，才便於他人評估。研究者可先提出範圍適中的問題：某個詞語在特定子句中有何作用？某個段落如何連接前後單元？隨後列明所採用的文本形式、相關上下文，以及從觀察走向結論的推理。「這個字的意思是某某」往往隱藏數項不同主張：詞形辨識是否正確？那個意思在所討論的時期與文體中是否可行？本處句法與上下文是否支持它？這些問題應分別受檢驗。0102

審核的目的，是使詮釋更負責任，而不是假裝所有爭論都可以消失。好的分析記錄最有力的替代解釋，說明甚麼觀察會支持它，並按證據強弱表達信心程度。歷史背景若尚未確定，不應變成毫無保留的神學前提；反過來說，缺乏絕對確定性亦不代表所有想像得到的讀法同樣合理。關鍵在於：各項解釋能否涵蓋資料，以及需要多少尚未證實的假設。03

向經文提出的問題本身也可能預先帶入答案。只有當兩個選項確實互相排斥，並且涵蓋合理可能性時，強迫二選一才有幫助。論文題目可能問某作者是「律法主義者還是放縱主義者」，但研究者須先界定這些分類，才能判斷任何一項是否適用。作者可能在論證不同部分處理不同問題，也可能拒絕兩個分類背後共同的假設，或根本關注另一議題。Carson 將問題的設定視為邏輯問題：問題可能預設了本應先證明的事項，也可能製造虛假的二分法。04

可供審核的研究問題會區分描述與評價。「作者在本段落提出甚麼對比？」促使讀者檢視文本關係；「作者為何在此拒絕一切律法？」卻已預設可能尚未成立的範圍。後一問題可以修訂：先查明所討論的是甚麼律法、做法、受眾或主張，再問「拒絕」「限定」或其他關係哪一個最能描述經文。好的問題也應說明甚麼證據會削弱自己偏好的結論。如果無論觀察到甚麼都不可能改變答案，這項研究可能只是重述詮釋者立場，而不是對立場進行檢驗。05

從證據走向結論

有說服力的主張還需要「論證保證」：為甚麼這類觀察足以支持這類結論？例如，詮釋者留意到某表達出現在段落首尾，並主張這種重複構成框架。兩次用詞可以核對，但框架主張比單純計數更進一步。研究者須注意單元從哪裏開始、在哪裏結束，該表達在首尾如何發揮作用，以及所提出的界線能否同時解釋人物、主題或論證的轉換。若該詞在全書到處常見，單憑它出現所得的框架證據，可能弱於某個不尋常表達在關鍵轉折處的重現。穩健的論證會說明連繫，同時不把罕見性當成絕對規則。06

這不是機械式評分表。某一特徵未獲支持，不會在其他證據強勁時自動否決讀法；反過來，列出多項薄弱平行也不會自動證明結論。研究者須區分彼此獨立的觀察與對同一觀察的重複描述。三個句子若都建立在同一個詞彙巧合之上，不會因使用不同術語就變成三個獨立見證。相反，句法、文學與上下文觀察若處理主張的不同面向，便可能提供互補支持。說明每項支持的性質，才能讓批評者在恰當的推論步驟提出異議。07

Lexical meaning and etymology 詞彙意義與語源

The root fallacy occurs when an interpreter assumes that a word's historical formation supplies its meaning in every later occurrence. Etymology can illuminate a word's history, and speakers may sometimes exploit the visible parts of a compound. Neither possibility removes the need to establish how the expression functions in the language and discourse in question. Compare uses from relevant periods and genres, examine the construction in which the word occurs, and ask whether a proposed nuance is independently signaled. A morphological breakdown is a hypothesis about word formation, not a complete contextual definition.08

Another danger is illegitimate totality transfer: importing the range of meanings documented in different contexts into every occurrence. Lexical resources help identify possibilities; they do not grant a reader permission to insert all possibilities into one sentence. Nor should a word be equated automatically with a whole theological concept. An author can develop an idea through clauses, images, narrative arrangement, and argument even where a familiar technical term is absent. This requires both lexical care and attention to discourse.0809

Historical development and anachronism

Historical investigation asks how a form and its uses developed; contextual interpretation asks what a form contributes to a particular ancient utterance. The questions interact, but the direction of time matters. A later meaning cannot be assumed to have existed in an earlier period merely because the same written form appears in both. The same caution applies when an English word ultimately descends from Greek. Shared ancestry may be interesting evidence about linguistic history, but it does not make a modern image available to an ancient author. Carson discusses the popular rendering of δύναμις by reference to modern dynamite as an example of importing a later association into an earlier text.10

In Romans 1:16 the clause δύναμις γὰρ θεοῦ ἐστιν εἰς σωτηρίαν supplies a claim about the gospel's relation to God's power and salvation. Nothing in the Greek form itself establishes a reference to modern explosives. A preacher might choose a contemporary analogy to convey force, but analogy and translation have different responsibilities. An analogy must be identified as the speaker's contemporary comparison and assessed for misleading implications. It should not be presented as a hidden literal meaning or as evidence that Paul intended an image unavailable in his setting. Even an analogy that communicates something vivid can mislead if its associations—destruction, for example—direct the audience away from the clause's stated movement toward salvation.11

Chronological control is not achieved simply by announcing that a source is “ancient.” A useful comparative example should be dated, located, and described with enough precision to show why it can inform the question. A fourth-century Christian use, a first-century inscription, and a modern translation may each be valuable for a different research purpose. They do not have the same evidentiary relationship to a first-century author. The later Christian use can illuminate reception; the inscription may illuminate a roughly contemporary usage in its own setting; the translation shows an interpreter's later decision. None can be substituted for careful analysis of the passage itself. Where the date or transmission history is disputed, the uncertainty should remain visible.1213

The safeguard is therefore a timeline with an explicit inferential arrow. Place the biblical passage, the comparative witness, and any proposed later semantic development in order. State whether the other witness is offered as evidence of a possible contemporary usage, a historical development, or subsequent reception. Then ask whether the claim actually requires access, direct influence, or only a shared linguistic possibility. This distinction makes room for historical interpretation without treating every later development as an error; it prevents a later meaning from becoming an unexamined premise about an earlier writer.10

Worked comparison: σάρξ in Romans and Luke

An audit becomes more than a list of warnings when it shows how an alternative reading changes the argument. Romans 8:6 contrasts τὸ φρόνημα τῆς σαρκός with τὸ φρόνημα τοῦ πνεύματος and associates the respective expressions with death and with life and peace. Luke 24:39, by contrast, places σάρκα alongside ὀστέα in a statement that a πνεῦμα does not have flesh and bones as the speaker does. These observations are directly checkable in the Greek clauses. They do not require the interpreter to suppose that Paul and Luke share an identical argumentative purpose, or that the English gloss “flesh” exhausts the meaning of either passage. The differences in co-text and construction make a theological description appropriate to one argument an unsafe automatic definition for every other occurrence.1415

That comparison must itself be kept within bounds. The contrast in Romans is part of a larger account of life according to flesh and Spirit; one should read the surrounding chapter before deciding exactly how “flesh” functions in Paul's argument. In Luke, the words “flesh and bones” belong to a scene in which the risen Jesus invites the disciples to look and touch. The pairing therefore provides contextual evidence for bodily reference, but it does not by itself settle every question about resurrection embodiment. This is a useful distinction between a conclusion that a particular clause supports and a larger claim that needs further argument. In a research paper the student should quote or identify the two clauses, state the proposed readings, and explain the inferential step instead of merely attaching the label “totality transfer” to an opponent.16

Campbell's discussion uses the contrast between Romans 8:6 and Luke 24:39 to illustrate why the full set of a word's possible uses should not be imported into each occurrence. He also warns that English glosses can carry associations not shared by the Greek expression. Such observations do not imply that context mechanically produces a single unquestionable translation. A reader must still assess syntax, collocations, genre, authorial usage, and the semantic possibilities attested in comparable material. Nor is a theological account forbidden from drawing together multiple passages: the problem arises when the resulting synthesis is passed off as the dictionary meaning of a word in each of its occurrences.14

所謂語根謬誤，是指詮釋者假設詞語的歷史構成在每一處後來用法中都決定其意思。語源研究可以照亮詞語歷史，作者有時亦可能刻意利用複合詞的構成；但這兩點都不能免除研究者考察該詞在特定語言和語篇中如何運作的責任。應比較時代及文類相關的例證、檢視其句法結構，並追問所提出的語義色彩有沒有獨立線索支持。拆解詞形可以提出構詞假設，卻不是現成的語境定義。08

另一個危險是「不合法的整體意義轉移」：把不同語境中記錄的所有詞義都帶進每一次出現。詞典協助研究者辨認可能範圍，卻不授權讀者將所有可能性同時塞進一個句子。詞語也不應自動等同完整的神學概念；作者即使沒有使用常見術語，也可以透過子句、意象、敘事安排和論證發展思想。因此，精確的詞彙研究必須配合語篇閱讀。0809

語義發展與時代錯置

歷史研究追問詞形與用法如何發展；語境詮釋則追問某個詞形在特定古代話語中發揮甚麼作用。兩種問題互相關聯，但時間方向不可倒轉。後來的詞義不能只因字形相同，就被假定已經存在於較早時期。英文詞語最終源自希臘文時，也需要同樣謹慎：共同語源可提供語言史上的有趣資料，卻不能使現代意象自動成為古代作者所能使用的意思。Carson 以將 δύναμις 聯繫現代炸藥的通俗譯法為例，說明把後世聯想倒灌進較早文本的問題。10

羅馬書一章十六節的 δύναμις γὰρ θεοῦ ἐστιν εἰς σωτηρίαν，論及福音與神的大能及救恩之間的關係。希臘文詞形本身沒有提供任何關於現代爆炸物的證據。講員可以選擇當代比喻傳達力量，但比喻與翻譯的責任不同：比喻必須清楚標示為講員自己的現代比較，並檢驗其中可能誤導人的聯想；它不可被當成隱藏的字面意思，也不可用來證明保羅有意使用他所處時代尚不存在的意象。即使生動的比喻確有溝通作用，若其毀壞性的聯想使聽眾忽略子句所指向的救恩，也仍可能造成誤解。11

只說一份材料「很古老」，並不足以控制年代問題。有用的比較例證需要標明年代、地點和文獻背景，讓讀者明白它為甚麼與研究問題有關。第四世紀基督徒的用法、第一世紀銘文和現代譯本，可能分別適合不同研究目的；但它們與第一世紀作者的證據關係並不相同。較晚的基督徒用法可以照亮接受史；銘文可能顯示大致同時期、特定處境中的用法；譯本則展現後世詮釋者的選擇。它們均不能取代對目標段落本身的細讀。如果材料的年代或傳抄歷史存在爭議，這種不確定性也應保持可見。1213

實際防護方法，是繪製一條有明確推論方向的時間線：依次放入經文、比較見證和任何所提出的後世語義發展；再說明另一份見證究竟是用來支持「當時可能有此用法」、描述「歷史上的語義變化」，還是考察「後來的接受」。最後追問該主張究竟需要作者接觸來源、直接受其影響，還是只需證明共享的語言可能性。這樣既能保留歷史解釋的空間，也不會把所有後來發展都視為錯誤；更重要的是，它阻止後世意義在未經檢驗下成為古代作者意思的前提。10

對照實例：羅馬書與路加福音中的 σάρξ

實際比較能使審核超越一張謬誤名稱清單，顯示不同讀法如何改變論證。羅馬書八章六節將 τὸ φρόνημα τῆς σαρκός 與 τὸ φρόνημα τοῦ πνεύματος 對照，分別連繫死亡、生命與平安。相反，路加福音二十四章三十九節將 σάρκα 與 ὀστέα 並列，指出 πνεῦμα 並沒有說話者所顯示的肉和骨。這些觀察可直接從希臘文子句核對；研究者不必假定保羅和路加在兩處有完全相同的論證目的，也不應以單一中文譯詞窮盡兩處意思。上下文與句法結構的差異表明：某處論證適合使用的神學描述，不能自動成為另一處的詞典定義。1415

這項比較本身也需要界限。羅馬書的對比屬於更廣闊的「隨從肉體」和「隨從聖靈」論述；確定該詞在保羅論證中的確切功能之前，應先細讀全章。路加的「肉和骨」則處於復活的耶穌邀請門徒觀看與觸摸的場景之中。兩個名詞並列支持身體指涉，但單憑這一句並不能解決復活身體的一切神學問題。這例子有助區分經文直接支持的結論，和需要更多論證才能成立的較大主張。研究生在論文中應列出兩個子句、說明所比較的讀法，並交代推論步驟，而非只給對方貼上「整體意義轉移」的標籤。16

Campbell 用羅馬書八章六節與路加福音二十四章三十九節的對照，說明不能將一個詞全部可能的用法帶進每個例證。他亦提醒讀者：英文對譯詞可能含有希臘文原詞所沒有的聯想。這些觀察並不表示語境會以機械方式產生唯一、無可爭議的譯法；研究者仍須檢查句法、搭配詞、文類、作者用法和可比較材料中的語義可能性。神學綜合當然可以把不同經文放在一起研究；問題出現在研究者將綜合的結果當作該詞在每個句子中的詞典意思。14

Synonyms and contextual evidence 同義詞與語境證據

John 21:15–17 offers a useful test case. Morphological analysis shows that Jesus' question in verse 15 includes ἀγαπᾷς, a present active indicative form of ἀγαπάω, while Peter's reply in verse 17 includes φιλῶ, a present active indicative form of φιλέω. Those observations identify forms; they do not establish that the two verbs encode two rigidly separate levels of love. To argue for a particular contrast, an interpreter must examine the sequence of questions and answers, other lexical variation in the scene, and relevant Johannine usage. A possible distinction should not be ruled out merely because the words overlap elsewhere; nor should it be assumed simply because the author uses two forms.1718

This distinction between identification and interpretation protects the value of original-language work. Morphology tells the reader what a form can be within a grammatical system. Context helps adjudicate its function. A translation, a lexicon, and a concordance are aids to that work, but each has a different purpose: the concordance locates occurrences, the lexicon organizes evidence about usage, and a translation makes choices for a target audience. None replaces close analysis of the passage.19

How to test a contrast

A responsible test begins with a table rather than a sermon point. Record each question and answer in order; distinguish the verb used in a question from the verb in the reply; then note changes in the accompanying commands, their objects, the participants' reactions, and the narrator's comments. Ask which differences are required by the immediate grammar and which are stylistic or potentially meaningful choices. The observation that a writer alternates forms is secure; the further conclusion that the alternation marks a doctrinal hierarchy is a hypothesis that requires an argument. Conversely, the hypothesis of stylistic variation must also explain the sequence and its effect in this particular dialogue. Neither a claim of contrast nor a claim of equivalence should be allowed to stand as an unexamined default.20

Campbell explains that two lexical expressions can overlap in some senses without being interchangeable in every context. This account avoids two equal and opposite shortcuts. One shortcut says that different words must always have different meanings, so a change of verb automatically marks a change of subject. The other says that because the words can overlap, their difference can never matter. The student instead asks whether the expressions can perform the same role in comparable constructions, what other contrasts the author uses, and whether the particular co-text gives a reader reason to notice a distinction. The burden of proof concerns this occurrence and the claimed effect, not an abstract claim that two dictionary entries are identical.21

Carson applies this problem to John 21 by discussing William Hendriksen's proposed distinction. Carson's criticism is narrower than a claim that lexical variation can never matter: he objects to establishing a distinction here simply because each verb has uses outside the range of the other. He draws attention to the variation in the accompanying commissions—feeding and shepherding, lambs and sheep—and argues that those differences should be weighed alongside the variation in the verbs for love. This is a substantive interpretation to be assessed against the text, not an instruction to dismiss every possible literary distinction. Its methodological contribution is to expose an inferential leap: different total ranges do not automatically establish different senses in a particular shared context.22

Consider the form of an inference: “The author uses A and B in a short sequence; therefore A always denotes a higher degree than B.” Its premise may be observable, but its conclusion contains an additional universal claim. Evidence for that universal would require much more than the sequence: an adequately sampled set of uses, control of context, and attention to counterexamples. A more restrained conclusion might say that an alternation is present and that interpreters disagree about its significance. If the proposed contrast is essential to the sermon's application, the preacher should say what textual evidence supports it and how the application changes if the verbs overlap in this context. A theological proposition built on the whole exchange may remain plausible even if a rigid lexical contrast is not established.23

約翰福音二十一章十五至十七節提供一個測試案例。希臘文詞法分析顯示，第十五節耶穌的問題包含 ἀγαπᾷς，這是 ἀγαπάω 的現在式、主動語態、直說語氣形式；第十七節彼得的回答包含 φιλῶ，這是 φιλέω 的現在式、主動語態、直說語氣形式。這些觀察辨認了詞形，卻不能單憑詞形證明兩個動詞必然代表兩種截然不同的愛。若要論證具體差別，研究者須考察三次問答的次序、場景中其他詞彙變化，以及約翰文獻的相關用法。別處存在語義重疊，不足以排除本處可能有區別；作者使用兩種形式，也不足以自動證明有區別。1718

區分「辨認」與「解釋」有助於保存原文研究的價值。詞法告訴讀者某個形式在語法系統中的可能身份；上下文則協助判斷其實際功能。譯本、詞典和經文彙編各有不同用途：彙編協助尋找出現位置，詞典整理用法證據，譯本則為目標讀者作出表達選擇；三者都不能取代對段落的細讀。19

如何檢驗詞義對比

負責任的測試應從表格開始，而非先決定講章重點。按次序記錄每次提問和回答，區分問題中的動詞與回答中的動詞，再記下相伴隨的命令、其受詞、人物反應以及敘述者的評語。哪些差異是當下句法所要求？哪些可能屬於文體變化或具有解釋意義的選擇？作者交替使用不同形式，是可以觀察的事實；但進一步宣稱這種交替標記神學上的高低階層，則是一項仍須論證的假設。反過來，「只是文體變化」的假設也應解釋整個對話中的次序和效果。不論主張對比還是等同，都不應成為未經檢驗的預設。20

Campbell 說明，兩個詞彙表達可以在某些詞義上重疊，卻不代表它們在所有語境中都能互換。這個分析避免兩種相反的捷徑。第一種捷徑認定不同詞語必定帶來不同意思，因此一旦動詞改變，主題也必然改變；第二種則認定詞語既然可以重疊，其差異便永遠不可能有意義。研究者應改問：在可比較的句法結構中，兩個表達能否發揮相同作用？作者還用了甚麼其他對照？本處上下文是否給讀者理由注意其差異？證明責任關乎這一次用法與所主張的效果，而非抽象地宣稱兩個詞典條目完全相同。21

Carson 討論 William Hendriksen 提出的區別，將同一問題應用於約翰福音二十一章。他的批評比「詞語變化永遠不重要」更具體：他反對單憑每個動詞有另一個動詞所沒有的用法，就確定兩者在本處必有差異。他指出，伴隨問答的囑咐也在「餵養／牧養」及「小羊／羊」之間變化，並認為這些變化應與愛的動詞變化一同衡量。這是一項應接受經文檢驗的具體解釋，並非命令讀者否定一切可能的文學差異；它在方法上的貢獻，是揭露一個推論跳躍：完整語義範圍不同，不會自動證明兩詞在同一處語境中的意思不同。22

例如，一個推論可能說：「作者在短段落中使用 A 和 B，因此 A 永遠比 B 代表更高程度。」前提或許可以觀察，但結論加入了額外的普遍主張。若要證成普遍規律，所需證據遠超過這一段落：必須有充分抽樣的例證、語境控制，以及對反例的認真考察。較審慎的結論可指出：文中確有詞語交替，但詮釋者對其意義存在分歧。若講道應用依賴某種對比，講員應說明其文本證據，也應交代若本處兩詞詞義重疊，應用會如何改變。建立於整段問答的神學命題，仍可能成立，即使僵硬的詞彙對比無法確證。23

Grammar, aspect, and competing models 語法、體貌與競爭模型

Verbal morphology matters, but it should not be converted into an automatic rule about the duration or theological finality of every action. Distinguish the form itself, the model used to describe its semantic contribution, the lexical character of the verb, and the contribution of context. Competing accounts of Greek aspect disagree on matters including the role of temporal reference and the classification of the perfect. An interpreter should name the model being used, show how it bears on the particular clause, and explain whether the conclusion would survive under a reasonable rival model.24

The same discipline applies to discourse prominence. A fronted expression or a particular tense-form may be evidence for an information-structural analysis, but “emphasis” is not a free-standing conclusion. What alternative ordering or form is being compared? What unit is prominent, and in relation to what? An interpretation gains force when its criteria can be applied to other examples and when apparent counterexamples are examined rather than ignored.2526

Aorist form and the scope of an inference

Romans 12:1 contains παραστῆσαι, identified by the Greek morphology data as an aorist active infinitive of παρίστημι. The grammatical identification is a starting observation. It is a further claim to say that the form alone commands an act that can happen only once and never again. Carson specifically criticizes deductions of a once-for-all commitment from the aorist form in this passage, along with other attempts to read an identical temporal or theological outcome directly from an aorist. His warning is not a prohibition on describing a commitment as decisive when the wider argument supports that description. It is a warning against making morphology carry an inference that has not been established by the clause and its context.2728

An analysis should show its levels. First, identify the form and its syntactic relation to the main clause. Second, state the account of aspect being used and distinguish a grammatical viewpoint from a description of how an event unfolds. Third, examine other clues, including the predicate, arguments, discourse setting, and surrounding exhortation. Finally, state the conclusion with the appropriate qualification. If contextual evidence supports a decisive response, cite that evidence. If the evidence cannot determine whether the response is repeated, do not claim that a tense label settles the question. A conclusion can be important without being derivable from a single morphological tag.29

The same reasoning protects against an opposite overcorrection. The fact that an aorist does not by itself specify “once for all” does not prove that the event is repeated, habitual, or insignificant. A specific passage can describe a unique event for reasons supplied by historical reference or co-text. The audit should therefore distinguish underdetermination from negation: “this form alone does not establish X” is not the same claim as “X is false.” Nor does an aspectual account remove all scholarly disagreement over temporal reference. When a technical model is contested, a writer should explain which conclusion depends on that model and which rests on observations shared by competing accounts.27

Syntax before slogans

A grammatical claim should identify its domain. Is the claim about a form's morphology, a relationship between clauses, or the information structure of a larger discourse? The same terminology should not be used for each without explanation. If a participle is said to express cause, for instance, the researcher should identify the main clause to which it is related, consider other possible relationships, and explain the contextual signals that favor a causal reading. Calling it “causal” in a translation is the conclusion of an analysis, not independent evidence that the analysis is correct. A comparison of alternative translations can reveal the decision but cannot by itself determine which translation is best.30

Information structure demands the same discipline. If a constituent appears early in a clause, record what precedes it, whether the construction imposes constraints on order, and how the constituent relates to information already introduced. Then distinguish the observation of position from a claim about focus or contrast. An interpreter who calls every initial expression “the main emphasis” deprives the analysis of a meaningful contrast class. A better account says what is highlighted relative to what alternatives and shows how that reading contributes to the surrounding argument. Where evidence does not discriminate between rival analyses, the resulting uncertainty belongs in the final account.31

動詞詞法具有重要意義，但不應被轉化成關於每個行動持續時間或神學終局性的自動規則。研究者需要區分詞形本身、描述其語義貢獻的理論模型、動詞的詞彙特徵，以及上下文帶來的作用。希臘文體貌的不同理論對時間指涉的地位及完成式的分類存在分歧。詮釋者應指出所採模型，說明它如何影響具體子句，並檢驗結論在合理的競爭模型下是否仍然成立。24

同樣的紀律也適用於語篇凸顯。前置成分或某個動詞形式可能支持資訊結構分析，但「強調」本身不是無須證明的結論。研究者應問：所比較的替代詞序或形式是甚麼？哪個單元相對於甚麼而凸顯？當分析準則可以用於其他例子，而且看似相反的資料也被認真處理時，解釋便更有說服力。2526

不定過去式與推論界限

羅馬書十二章一節包含 παραστῆσαι；希臘文詞法資料將它辨認為 παρίστημι 的不定過去式主動語態不定詞。詞法辨認是起點，而「這個形式本身命令一個只能發生一次、永不能再次發生的行動」則是額外主張。Carson 具體批評有人從本節的不定過去形式推導一次過、永不重複的委身，也批評把完全相同的時間或神學結果直接套進其他不定過去形式。這並不是說整段論證若提供充分理由，便不能描述某種決斷；他的警告是不要讓詞法承擔子句與語境尚未證成的推論。2728

分析應顯示不同層次。首先辨認詞形及其與主句的句法關係；其次說明所採用的體貌理論，區分語法視角與事件在現實中如何展開的描述；再檢查述語、論元、語篇處境和周邊勸勉等其他線索；最後按證據程度陳述結論。如果語境證據支持決斷性的回應，應指出該證據；如果證據不能決定回應是否重複，就不要宣稱一個時態標籤已解決問題。重要結論不必然能從單一詞法標記推導出來。29

同樣的推理也能防止矯枉過正。「不定過去式本身不能證明一次過」不等於證明事件一定重複、習慣性或不重要。某個段落可以因歷史指涉或上下文提供的其他理由，描述一次獨特事件。因此審核要區分「證據不足以決定」與「已證明否定」：「此形式單獨不能建立 X」不等於「X 是錯的」。體貌分析也不會消除一切有關時間指涉的學術分歧。當技術模型有爭議時，作者應說明哪些結論依賴該模型，哪些則建立在競爭模型共同接受的觀察之上。27

在口號之前先檢驗句法

語法主張應先說明其分析範圍：所討論的是詞形、子句之間的關係，還是較大語篇的資訊結構？不應不加解釋便把同一術語套用於每個層次。例如，若說某分詞表達原因，就應指出它與哪個主句相關、考慮其他可能關係，並說明哪些語境訊號支持因果讀法。在譯文中把它譯成「因為」是分析的結論，而不是該分析正確的獨立證據。比較不同譯本可以暴露譯者所作的決定，卻不能單靠數量決定哪個譯法最好。30

資訊結構也需要同樣紀律。如果某成分出現在子句前部，研究者應記錄它前面有甚麼、句法結構是否限制詞序，以及它與先前已引入資訊的關係。然後區分位置這項觀察，與焦點或對比這項解釋。如果詮釋者把每個句首成分都叫做「主要強調」，分析便失去有意義的對照類別。較好的說明會指出該成分相對於哪些選項被凸顯，並解釋這種讀法如何服務周邊論證。若證據無法區分競爭分析，最終報告就應保留這種不確定性。31

Comparison without parallelomania 比較研究與避免平行狂熱

Resemblance, shared cultural convention, and demonstrable literary dependence are different claims. Comparative work should first describe each text in its own setting, then state the precise feature compared and the proposed relationship. A common image can illuminate a shared repertoire without proving that one author directly borrowed from another. Distinctiveness, prominence within each work, chronology, access, and relevant differences all matter; no single criterion mechanically settles the question.3233

Compare not only similarities but also omissions and divergent functions. Two texts may use related language to advance different arguments, address different audiences, or locate an image in different narrative structures. Responsible comparison also resists the opposite temptation: declaring a tradition incomparable before the evidence has been examined. The goal is a controlled comparison with a stated purpose and a conclusion proportionate to the available evidence.34

What exactly has a parallel shown?

Suppose two texts depict a banquet in which a powerful host rewards a guest. The shared motif makes comparison possible, but the conclusion depends on the question. If the research question concerns the narrative function of hospitality, the two stories may be compared without proving that one author read the other. If the question is direct literary borrowing, stronger historical and textual evidence is required. A reader must ask when each text was produced, whether access is plausible, how distinctive the shared formulation is, and whether the sequence of elements is more specific than a widespread social convention. A table of similarities is a starting point; it is not an argument for dependence until the proposed relationship is justified.35

Gathercole explicitly distinguishes resemblance from relation and considers distinctiveness, salience, and absences in comparative work. The distinction protects both kinds of scholarship. It permits comparison where dependence is uncertain, while preventing similarity alone from bearing the weight of a historical claim. A salient feature matters because it has an identifiable role within a text, not merely because a modern reader can find it. Distinctiveness is comparative as well: a rare sequence may carry different evidentiary weight from a common formula, but rarity itself should be established in a relevant corpus rather than asserted. A proposed absence should likewise be described carefully: the absence of a word is not automatically the absence of the concept, and absence from surviving evidence is not proof that no historical form existed.36

The conclusion of a comparison should name its level. “Both narratives use a banquet scene” is a descriptive claim. “Both employ the scene to challenge social rank” is a functional claim requiring analysis of each narrative. “The later writer deliberately reworked the earlier story” is a claim about historical relationship and intention requiring evidence of a different kind. These claims can all be valuable, but moving from the first to the third without additional argument is the very step a critical audit should expose. Conversely, failure to establish borrowing does not make the functional comparison useless: it can still clarify assumptions, narrative decisions, and the range of ancient cultural practices that the interpreter should consider.37

Record differences as carefully as similarities

The order in which evidence is presented can bias a comparative argument. A list of similarities followed by a declaration of dependence may leave the reader unaware that the two texts assign opposite roles to the same image. Prepare the comparison in parallel columns: identify a shared element, describe its immediate setting in each text, and record a difference in function where one exists. Then ask which features matter to the particular hypothesis. A difference need not disprove borrowing; authors may transform inherited material. But a proposed transformation cannot be invoked as an unlimited explanation for every mismatch. The account should say why this change is plausible in the later work and what other features make a relationship likely enough to investigate.3839

Chronology is a necessary control for claims about direction. If text A is demonstrably later than text B, that fact is compatible with A's use of B, but it does not prove access or borrowing. If their dates overlap or are disputed, the direction may be unresolved. A shared earlier tradition, oral circulation, a common setting, and independent use of a familiar motif can also explain resemblance in some cases. These alternatives should be assessed rather than merely named: which observed details does each explain, and what additional assumptions does it require? An argument need not eliminate every theoretical possibility to be persuasive, but it should not treat the preferred solution as the only imaginable one.40

The same controls apply to disagreements between modern readers about an allusion. One reader may regard a sequence of rare verbal correspondences as sufficient; another may regard the material as conventional or the proposed source as too remote. Their disagreement may concern the description of the data, the relevant comparison set, or the threshold for historical dependence. Stating which issue is disputed turns an exchange of labels into a testable discussion. A theological interpretation of a proposed echo should also be distinguished from the prior identification of the echo: even when a relationship is likely, its precise significance for a later argument remains a further interpretive question.41

相似、共同文化慣例，以及可以論證的文學依賴，是不同層次的主張。比較研究應先在各自處境中描述文本，再明確指出比較的特徵和所主張的關係。共同意象可以顯示共享的文化資源，卻不能單憑自身證明某作者直接借用另一文本。獨特性、該特徵在各文本中的重要程度、年代、接觸可能性與相關差異都需要考慮；沒有任何單一準則可以機械式地解決全部問題。3233

比較不僅要考察相似，也要注意缺席與不同功能。兩份文本可能使用相近語言，卻服務於不同論證、面向不同讀者，或置於不同敘事結構中。負責任的比較同樣要避免相反的誘惑：在檢驗證據之前便宣稱某一傳統根本無法比較。目標是說明比較目的、控制推論範圍，並讓結論與可得證據相稱。34

所謂平行究竟證明甚麼？

設想兩份文本都描寫一場筵席，其中有權勢的主人賞賜客人。共同主題使比較成為可能，但結論取決於研究問題。若研究問題是款待在敘事中的作用，即使無法證明一位作者讀過另一篇作品，也可以比較兩個故事。若問題是直接文學借用，就需要更強的歷史與文本證據。研究者須問：兩份文本何時產生？作者接觸另一文本是否合理？共同措辭有多獨特？各元素的次序是否比普遍的社會慣例更具體？相似點表格只是起點；在論證所主張的關係之前，它尚未成為文學依賴的證據。35

Gathercole 明確區分「相似」與「關係」，並在比較研究中討論獨特性、顯著性和缺席。這項區分同時保障兩種學術工作：它容許研究者在依賴關係不確定時仍作比較，也避免單靠相似承擔歷史主張的重量。一個特徵之所以顯著，是因為它在文本中有可辨認的功能，不只是現代讀者能夠找到它。獨特性也需要比較：罕見的元素組合可能比常見公式具有不同的證據分量，但研究者須在相關文獻範圍內證明其罕見，而非單純宣稱。所謂「缺席」同樣須仔細說明：沒有某個詞不等於沒有相關概念；現存證據沒有某種形式，也不等於證明歷史上從未存在。36

比較的結論應明確說出其層次。「兩段敘事都使用筵席場景」是描述性主張；「兩者都用筵席挑戰社會階序」是功能性主張，需要分別分析每個敘事；「後來的作者有意重寫較早的故事」則是有關歷史關係和意圖的主張，需要另一類證據。三種主張都可能有價值，但若沒有額外論證便由第一項跳到第三項，正是批判性審核應揭露的步驟。反過來說，未能證明借用，也不表示功能比較沒有用處：比較仍能釐清假設、敘事選擇，以及研究者應考慮的古代文化實踐範圍。37

同樣細心記錄差異與相似

證據的呈現次序可能使比較論證出現偏差。一串相似點之後立即宣稱依賴，可能使讀者沒有留意到兩份文本賦予同一意象相反功能。可用平行欄準備比較：列出共同元素，描述它在各文本中的直接處境，並記錄存在的功能差異；然後追問哪些特徵與所測試的假設有關。差異不必然推翻借用，因為作者可能轉化已有材料；但「作者改寫了來源」也不能成為解釋所有不相符之處的無限通行證。研究者應說明這種改變在後來作品中為何合理，以及其他哪些特徵使兩者關係值得探究。3839

對方向性的主張而言，年代是必要控制。如果文本 A 可以確定比文本 B 晚，這與 A 使用 B 的可能性相容，卻不能證明 A 有機會接觸 B 或實際借用。若日期重疊或存在爭議，方向可能仍未解決。共同的較早傳統、口傳、共享環境和各自使用熟悉主題，也可能在某些情況下解釋相似。這些替代方案應接受評估，而不只是被列名：每個方案能解釋哪些觀察？需要增加甚麼假設？有說服力的論證不必消除所有理論可能性，但不應把偏好的解釋說成唯一可以想像的方案。40

現代讀者對經文回響的分歧也適用同樣控制。一位讀者可能認為連續出現罕見詞語足以支持回響，另一位則可能認為材料屬慣例，或認為所提出的來源距離太遠。雙方可能爭論的是資料如何描述、相關比較範圍，或確立歷史依賴所需的門檻。指明實際爭點，才能把標籤式爭論轉化為可檢驗討論。對某個回響的神學解釋，也應與先行辨認回響的工作區分：即使兩份文本可能相關，這種關係對後來論證究竟有甚麼意義，仍是另一項需要解釋的問題。41

An auditable research record 可審核的研究紀錄

For each consequential claim, record the passage and textual witness, the relevant observation, the inference, a plausible alternative, and the source actually consulted. Distinguish a direct ancient witness from a modern scholar's report about it. Where a precise page, edition, or quotation cannot be checked, leave that detail open instead of reconstructing it from memory. A source marker demonstrates that a retrieval system associated material with an answer; it does not by itself show that the source says what the answer claims.42

The final synthesis should retain the result of the audit. Claims strongly supported by the relevant evidence can be stated confidently; contested reconstructions should name their alternatives; unsupported assertions should be revised or removed. This procedure makes disagreement productive: another reader can inspect the same evidence and identify the precise point at which interpretation diverges.43

A claim ledger and a fair account of disagreement

The research record can be organized as a table with six columns: claim, witness, observation, inference, rival explanation, and confidence. In the witness column identify the edition, manuscript, ancient text, inscription, or modern work actually consulted. In the observation column write what is visible there before adding the interpretive conclusion. In the inference column explain why the observation supports the claim. The rival column names a serious alternative and the strongest evidence for it; it is not a place to caricature another interpreter. The confidence column states whether the conclusion is well supported, contested, or presently unsubstantiated. These labels summarize an argument; they do not replace it.44

The distinction between primary and secondary evidence depends on the question. In an exegetical study of a biblical passage, the biblical text is a primary object of analysis and a modern commentary is a secondary interpretation. In a study of the reception of that commentary, the commentary itself becomes a primary object. A research handout from Fuller Theological Seminary emphasizes distinguishing these types of source, making an explicit thesis, and defending it with relevant evidence. This clarification matters when an article reports what another scholar says about an ancient inscription: the report is evidence for that author's account, but the researcher must consult the inscription itself before claiming direct examination. If direct examination is not possible, an accurate “as reported by” attribution preserves the chain.45

The ledger can also identify where a disagreement actually lies. Two scholars might agree on the form of a clause while proposing different functions for it. Another pair might share an interpretation of the clause but disagree over whether a later text intentionally echoes it. A third disagreement may concern the ethical implications of an interpretation rather than the interpretation itself. Recording the point of divergence prevents the researcher from multiplying apparent disputes or asserting that a scholar denies an observation the scholar in fact accepts. It also makes revision efficient: when the source or inference changes, the researcher can locate the downstream conclusions that relied on it.46

For classroom use, ask a peer to reconstruct the argument from the record without access to the author's oral explanation. Can the peer find the source at the stated location? Does the cited passage actually support the proposition for which it is used? Is a paraphrase presented fairly, or has a nuanced suggestion become an unqualified consensus? Can the peer articulate the best alternative without adding unstated assumptions? These questions turn verification into a reproducible practice. They also protect scholarly disagreement: a conclusion may remain disputed after all parties have checked the evidence, and a responsible handbook should disclose that fact rather than using the appearance of a footnote to create false certainty.4547

Revising an argument after a failed check

An audit is valuable only if its results can change the work. When a source does not support a claim, the first task is to identify the extent of the problem. If the claim is peripheral, correct or remove the sentence and review any footnote attached to it. If it is a premise for the thesis, revisit the argument as a whole. Replace the unsupported premise with evidence that actually bears on the question, narrow the thesis, or explain that the evidence does not decide the issue. A correction in a footnote is insufficient when the main paragraph still states the original claim without qualification. The wording, the evidence, and the conclusion must agree.48

The same principle applies when a quotation is accurate but its context changes its force. A scholar may present a position only to reject it, describe a hypothesis as provisional, or summarize another author's view. A selective quotation can conceal all three possibilities. Before using it, read enough before and after the cited material to determine the speaker, the claim's role, and any qualification. In a paraphrase, retain the original level of confidence and identify the actual author of the position. If the source's argument turns on an exception, the paraphrase should not erase it merely to make a sentence more fluent. Careful attribution is part of reasoning because it tells readers what the evidence can and cannot show.49

Keep a version of the initial question and record why the final formulation changed. A revision trail helps a reader see whether the study followed evidence or silently redefined its target after encountering contrary evidence. It also gives teachers a constructive way to assess research: correction is evidence of learning, not automatically evidence of failure. At publication, a compact note may be sufficient, while the fuller record remains available for review. The published claim should make clear which propositions have textual support, which remain hypotheses, and which alternatives deserve further study. The aim is a conclusion that can be challenged precisely because its route is visible.50

對每項影響結論的重要主張，應記錄經文和文本見證、相關觀察、推論、合理替代解釋，以及實際查閱的來源。要區分古代文獻的直接證據與現代學者對它的轉述。若無法核實確切頁碼、版本或引文，就應暫時保留該欄，而不是憑記憶補寫。檢索系統提供的來源標記，僅顯示系統將某材料與答案連結；標記本身不證明該來源確實支持答案的主張。42

最終綜合也應保留審核結果。獲相關證據充分支持的主張可以清楚陳述；有爭議的歷史重建應列明替代方案；缺乏支持的論斷則應修訂或刪除。此程序能使分歧更有建設性：其他讀者可以檢視同一組證據，指出雙方究竟在哪個推論步驟出現分歧。43

主張紀錄與公平呈現分歧

研究紀錄可整理成六欄：主張、見證、觀察、推論、競爭解釋與信心程度。「見證」欄應列出實際查閱的版本、抄本、古代文獻、銘文或現代著作；「觀察」欄先記錄其中可見的資料，再加入詮釋性結論；「推論」欄說明這些觀察為何支持主張。「競爭解釋」欄應列出有力的替代讀法及其最佳證據，而不是歪曲另一位學者的立場；「信心程度」欄則指出結論是充分支持、仍有爭議，或目前缺乏證據。這些標記只能概括論證，不能取代論證本身。44

一手與二手證據的區分取決於研究問題。若研究某段聖經經文，聖經文本是分析的一手對象，現代註釋書則屬二手詮釋；若研究該註釋書的接受史，註釋書本身便成為一手對象。富勒神學院一份研究論文指引強調區分來源類型、清楚陳述論題，並以相關證據支持論證。當文章轉述另一位學者對古代銘文的看法時，這個區分尤其重要：轉述可證明該作者如何報告資料；但若研究者聲稱自己直接檢查了銘文，就需要實際查閱銘文。若未能直接查閱，應以「據某某報告」清楚保留證據鏈。45

紀錄表亦能顯示分歧真正發生的位置。兩位學者可能同意子句的詞形，卻對其功能提出不同解釋；另一組學者可能同意子句的解釋，卻對後來文本是否有意回響該段落持不同意見；第三種分歧可能關乎某種詮釋的倫理含義，而非詮釋本身。記錄分歧點能避免製造表面的爭論，也能避免誤稱某學者否定了其實接受的觀察。當來源或推論有所變更時，研究者亦可迅速找出依賴它的後續結論。46

課堂練習可請同儕在沒有作者口頭補充的情況下，單憑紀錄重建論證。同儕能否按所列位置找到來源？所引段落是否真的支持被引用來支持的命題？意譯是否公平，還是把謹慎的建議說成無條件的共識？同儕能否不加進隱藏假設，就說明最有力的替代讀法？這些問題使驗證成為可重複的實踐，也保護學術分歧：即使各方均已檢查資料，結論仍可能存有爭議；負責任的手冊應交代這一點，而非利用註腳的外觀製造虛假確定性。4547

核查未通過後如何修訂論證

審核只有在結果能改變作品時才有價值。當來源不支持某項主張，首先要判斷問題範圍：若該主張只是次要細節，可修正或刪除該句，並檢查相連的註腳；若它是論題的必要前提，就須重新檢視整體論證。作者可以用真正相關的證據取代未獲支持的前提、縮小論題範圍，或說明現有資料不足以裁定。若正文仍毫無保留地陳述原來主張，單在註腳加上一句修正並不足夠；正文措辭、證據與結論必須一致。48

引文逐字正確，但上下文改變其作用時，也適用同一原則。學者可能提出某立場正是為了反駁它，可能把假設描述為暫時性建議，也可能只是在概括別人的觀點；選擇性引文可以掩蓋這三種情況。引用前應閱讀足夠的前後文，確認說話者、主張在論證中的角色，以及任何限定條件。意譯時保留原文的信心程度，並正確指出立場的作者。如果原作者的論證依賴某個例外，不應為了行文流暢而刪去。準確歸屬本身就是推理的一部分，因為它告訴讀者證據能證明甚麼、不能證明甚麼。49

保留最初的研究問題，並記錄最終表述為何改變。修訂紀錄有助讀者判斷研究是否跟隨證據，還是遇到反證後悄悄重新定義目標；它也讓教師以建設性方式評估研究，因為修正可以是學習的證據，而非自動等同失敗。發表時簡短註記可能已足夠，較完整的紀錄則可供審查。公開主張應清楚區分哪些命題有文本支持、哪些仍是工作假設，以及哪些替代解釋值得進一步研究。目標是令結論因推論路徑可見，而能被精確檢驗。50
