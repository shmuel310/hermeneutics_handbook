01 · EXEGETICAL WORKFLOW ARCHITECTURE 01 · 綜合釋經流程架構

Comparative Architecture of Comprehensive Exegetical Workflows 綜合聖經釋經流程之比較架構與操作序位

● METHODOLOGICAL AXIOM · THE OUTLINE-RESEARCH SPIRAL 方法論公理 · 大綱與研究之螺旋進路

Comprehensive exegesis is neither a mechanical checklist nor an undisciplined intuition; it is a disciplined spiral moving from independent primary-language reading and textual establishment through syntax, discourse, and historical context to critical commentary dialogue and theological synthesis. 綜合釋經既非機械式的核對清單，亦非毫無章法的直覺臆測；它是一個嚴謹的方法論螺旋，從獨立的原文閱讀與文本確立出發，依序推進至句法、語篇、歷史處境與批判性註釋書對話，最終達致聖經神學的整合。

1.1 Convergence Across Contemporary Exegetical Handbooks

A decisive consensus characterizes contemporary postgraduate biblical methodology: valid interpretation requires an ordered sequence of analytical operations rather than an ad hoc assortment of historical, lexical, or homiletical observations.01 Across standard primers—Gordon D. Fee's New Testament Exegesis, Douglas K. Stuart's Old Testament Exegesis, Michael J. Gorman's Elements of Biblical Exegesis, Grant R. Osborne's The Hermeneutical Spiral, Craig L. Blomberg and Jennifer Foutz Markley's A Handbook of New Testament Exegesis, and Walter C. Kaiser Jr.'s Toward an Exegetical Theology—scholars articulate distinct pedagogical sequences that nonetheless converge on a five-phase operational architecture.02

In Phase 1 (Textual Delimitation and Establishment), the exegete isolates a coherent literary pericope, establishes its boundaries via structural markers (inclusio, spatial/temporal shifts, or formulaic transitions), and performs an independent provisional translation directly from critical editions (NA28 or BHS) prior to consulting secondary literature.03 Textual criticism precedes grammatical analysis because one cannot validly parse a clause whose wording remains unverified against external manuscript witnesses (papyri, uncials, versions, and patristic citations) and internal transcriptional and intrinsic probabilities.04 As Eldon Jay Epp and P. Kyle McCarter Jr. demonstrate, textual criticism is never a mechanical counting of manuscripts; it is an historical-philological reconstruction that accounts for how scribal harmonizations, omissions, or theological clarifications arose in the transmission of the text.

1.2 From Morpho-Syntax and Discourse to Historical Context

Phase 2 (Morpho-Syntactical and Lexical Analysis) interrogates the primary text's grammatical architecture. Following Stanley E. Porter and Jeffrey T. Reed, advanced exegesis pairs micro-level clausal parsing—cases, prepositions, verbal aspect, and subordination—with macro-level discourse analysis (semantic structure analysis) to prevent atomizing a text into isolated propositions.05 Diagramming or sentence-flowing separates independent kernel clauses from subordinate modifiers so that the author's primary line of argument remains visible. Lexical semantics relies on critical lexica (BDAG, HALOT) and synchronic usage across contemporaneous corpora rather than concordances, English-word-study tools, or etymological speculation.06

Phase 3 (Literary, Genre, and Rhetorical Criticism) identifies macro- and micro-genres (historical narrative, epistolary paraenesis, prophetic oracle, covenant lawsuit, apocalyptic) alongside ancient rhetorical dispositio (exordium, narratio, propositio, probatio, peroratio) and narratological poetics (plot, characterization, focalization, and implied reader). Phase 4 (Historical-Cultural and Comparative Context) situates the pericope within its Ancient Near Eastern, Second Temple Jewish, or Greco-Roman Mediterranean milieu—engaging primary witnesses such as the Septuagint (LXX), Dead Sea Scrolls, Philo, Josephus, and Hellenistic moralists (e.g., Plutarch, Seneca) while treating them as active cultural context in which the biblical author participated rather than detached background.07

1.3 Methodological Divergences: Timing, Logic, and Theological Horizon

Despite broad architectural agreement, handbooks diverge on three critical procedural questions:08

  • Deferred vs. Concurrent Commentary Consultation: Fee, Stuart, and Downs/Green urge deferring secondary commentaries until independent translation and grammatical parsing are complete, guarding the exegete's initial textual instincts from premature interpretive bias. Conversely, some practitioners consult critical commentary series early (ICC, Hermeneia, NIGTC, WBC, Anchor Bible, BECNT, NICNT) to flag textual cruxes and alternative viewpoints before forming rigid hypotheses, then alternate dialectically between primary text and secondary literature along an "outline-research spiral."
  • Inductive Data-Gathering vs. Deductive Thesis-Testing: D. A. Carson distinguishes inductive research (organizing textual data to resolve a specific exegetical crux) from deductive research (testing a sharp hypothesis across a corpus). Inductive work yields balanced description yet risks producing well-organized data without a persuasive central thesis, whereas deductive work achieves thesis clarity yet risks "domesticating the evidence" to protect a favored claim.
  • Historical-Critical Description vs. Theological Interpretation of Scripture (TIS): Porter's Handbook privileges philological, socio-scientific, and historical description, whereas Gorman, Kaiser, and Markus Bockmuehl integrate reception history (Wirkungsgeschichte), canonical trajectory, and ecclesial proclamation directly into Phase 5 (Theological Synthesis), insisting that critical exegesis must culminate in the text's theological claim upon the believing community.09

1.1 當代釋經手冊之方法論共識與五階段架構

當代神學研究院層級的聖經方法論存在一項決定性共識：有效的文本詮釋必須依循有序的分析步驟，而非零散拼湊歷史背景、字義考察或講道感想。01 檢視當代標準釋經手冊——包括戈登·費依（Gordon D. Fee）的《新約釋經手冊》、道格拉斯·斯圖爾特（Douglas K. Stuart）的《舊約釋經手冊》、麥可·戈爾曼（Michael J. Gorman）的《聖經釋經要素》、格蘭特·奧斯本（Grant R. Osborne）的《詮釋學螺旋》、克雷格·布隆伯格與珍妮佛·馬克利（Craig L. Blomberg & Jennifer Foutz Markley）的《新約釋經手冊》，以及華特·凱澤（Walter C. Kaiser Jr.）的《邁向釋經神學》——儘管各家在教學步驟編排上略有差異，卻一致收斂於一個五階段的操作架構。02

在第一階段（經文界定與文本確立），釋經者首先劃定連貫的文學段落（Pericope），依據結構標記確認邊界，並在查閱任何次級註釋書之前，直接從原文批判版（NA28 或 BHS）進行獨立試譯。03 文本鑑別學（Textual Criticism）必須優先於語法分析，因為若連經文的原初用字尚未透過外證抄本（蒲草紙、大寫體、古譯本）與內證抄寫機率加以確立，後續的句法解析便無從談起。04

1.2 從形態句法、語篇結構邁向歷史處境

第二階段（形態句法與詞彙語義分析）深入剖析原文的語法結構。承續史丹利·波特（Stanley E. Porter）與傑弗瑞·里德（Jeffrey T. Reed）的語言學進路，高階釋經將微觀的子句解析（格位、介詞、動詞體貌與主從結構）與宏觀的語篇分析（Discourse Analysis / 語義結構分析）緊密結合，藉以防止將連貫論述割裂為孤立的命題。05 在詞彙層面，學者必須依賴權威學術辭典（如 BDAG、HALOT）並考察共時語境用法，嚴禁依賴簡易經文彙編或詞源學臆測。06

第三階段（文學體裁與修辭批判）辨識經文的宏觀與微觀文體（歷史敘事、書信勸勉、先知神諭、天啟文學），並剖析古代修辭編排（dispositio：開端 exordium、敘述 narratio、命題 propositio、論證 probatio、結語 peroratio）。第四階段（歷史文化與比較處境）將段落置於古代近東、第二聖殿猶太教或希羅地中海社會實況中，直接對話《七十士譯本》、死海古卷、斐羅與約瑟夫斯等一手文獻，並視之為作者身處的動態文化「處境」（context），而非靜態抽離的「背景」（background）。07

1.3 方法論分歧：查閱時機、邏輯進路與神學視域

儘管各家手冊在核心架構上高度一致，但在三個關鍵操作議題上仍存在方法論張力：08

  • 延後查閱與同步對話之爭： 費依、斯圖爾特與唐斯／格林（Downs & Green）主張在完成獨立翻譯與句法分析後才查閱次級註釋書，以防讀者被詮釋史預先染色；反之，部分學者主張早期瀏覽批判性註釋書（如 ICC、Hermeneia、NIGTC、WBC、Anchor Bible），以迅速鎖定釋經難題（cruces），再回到原文進行辯證檢驗。
  • 歸納式資料彙整與演繹式論題檢驗： 卡森（D. A. Carson）區分了「歸納研究」（累積文本證據以解決難題）與「演繹研究」（提出假設並跨文本檢驗）。前者較為持平卻易流於資料羅列而缺乏論點鋒芒；後者論題鮮明，卻極易陷入「馴化證據」（domesticating the evidence）的誘惑，刻意曲解經文以維護預設論點。
  • 歷史批判描述與神學詮釋（TIS）： 波特編纂的《新約釋經手冊》側重語文學、社會科學與歷史描述；而戈爾曼、凱澤與博克穆爾（Markus Bockmuehl）則主張將接受史（Wirkungsgeschichte）、正典軌跡與教會宣講直接納入第五階段的「神學綜合」。09

The Six-Tier Hierarchy of Exegetical Evidence 釋經證據之六層權威階層與同心圓控管

● EVIDENTIARY RULE · CONCENTRIC AUTHORITY 證據法則 · 同心圓權威遞減律

Not all exegetical observations carry equal probative weight. Evidentiary authority is stratified concentrically: primary original-language syntax (Tier 1) and immediate cotext (Tier 2) exercise veto power over macro-structural hypotheses (Tier 3), intertextual echoes (Tier 4), historical parallels (Tier 5), and secondary commentaries (Tier 6). 並非所有釋經觀察皆具同等證明力。證據權威依同心圓分層：原文句法（第一層）與直接上下文（第二層）對宏觀結構假設（第三層）、正典互涉回聲（第四層）、歷史平行文獻（第五層）及次級註釋書（第六層）擁有絕對的否決權。

2.1 Concentric Stratification: Why Evidentiary Tiers Are Ordered

Rigorous biblical exegesis requires not merely gathering data across multiple disciplines, but adjudicating conflicts when structural diagrams, historical parallels, or theological traditions pull in competing directions.10 To prevent methodological arbitrariness, the Six-Pillar Method enforces a six-tier hierarchy of exegetical evidence structured as concentric contextual circles.11 The closer an evidentiary stratum lies to the author's concrete linguistic encoding, the greater its semantic determinacy; as inquiry moves outward toward canonical, comparative, and reception horizons, evidence becomes progressively heuristic and subordinate. An interpreter who permits an outer circle—such as a Second Temple parallel or a confessional locus—to override an inner circle such as clausal syntax or immediate cotext inverts the epistemic order of human communication.

2.2 Tiers 1–2: Primary Text, Morpho-Syntax, and Immediate Cotext

Tier 1 (Primary Original-Language Text, Morphology, and Syntax) constitutes the foundational anchor of all interpretation. Before any semantic claim is advanced, textual criticism establishes the most plausible authorial wording.12 Thereafter, clausal syntax—grammatical subjects, verbal predicates, case relations, prepositional governance, and subordination (causal, telic, conditional, temporal, or concessive clauses)—sets non-negotiable boundaries on what a sentence can signify. Contemporary Greek linguistics (Stanley E. Porter, Buist M. Fanning, K. L. McKay) demonstrates that verbal morphology encodes aspect (the author's subjective viewpoint: perfective/aorist as whole action; imperfective/present-imperfect as ongoing process; stative/perfect as resulting state) rather than mechanical temporal Aktionsart.13 If an interpretation violates Tier 1 syntax, no appeal to macro-structure or cultural background can salvage it.

Tier 2 (Immediate Literary Context / Cotext) operates as the decisive arbiter of lexical polysemy.14 Words possess broad semantic ranges in isolation, yet within a concrete syntagmatic chain of clauses, the immediate cotext selects and activates one determinate sense (as Anthony Thiselton notes via Erhardt Güttgemanns's analysis of Rom 1:17, or Jesus's messianic self-disclosure in John 4:26 governed by v. 25). Logical connectives (γάρ, οὖν, ἀλλά, διὰ τοῦτο) bind adjacent propositions into causal, inferential, or adversative relations that govern paragraph logic and prohibit lifting clauses out of their argumentative flow.15

2.3 Tiers 3–6: Macro-Structure, Intertextuality, History, and Commentaries

Tier 3 (Macro-Structure and Book Argument) situates the pericope within the author's overarching compositional design (scopus libri), such as Matthew's fivefold discourse formula (καὶ ἐγένετο ὅτε ἐτέλεσεν), Genesis toledot headings (תּוֹלְדֹת), or Pauline rhetorical dispositio. Yet as J. P. Fokkelman warns, imposed chiastic schemas must never override Tier 1–2 linear syntax.16

Tier 4 (Canonical Intertextuality and Vorlage) traces inner-biblical exegesis (Michael Fishbane, Richard B. Hays, Abner Chou) using strict controls—availability, volume of verbal echo, and thematic coherence—while preserving E. D. Hirsch's boundary between determinate original meaning and unfolding canonical significance.17 Tier 5 (Historical, Cultural, and Second Temple Parallels) reconstructs the Ancient Near Eastern "cultural river" (John H. Walton) or Greco-Roman/Second Temple social matrix (N. T. Wright, Michael F. Bird), yet remains subordinate to explicit textual cotext because historical reconstructions rest on fragmentary artifacts and variable probabilities. Finally, Tier 6 (Secondary Literature and Confessional Traditions) serves a ministerial, dialogical role: in historic Protestant hermeneutics, Scripture remains norma normans non normata, whereas commentaries and dogmatic systems stand as subordinate norma normata.18 Citing a commentator's verdict without verifying its Tier 1–2 linguistic warrant commits an uncritical appeal to authority.

2.4 Adjudicating Evidentiary Conflicts in Practice

In concrete seminar practice, evidentiary conflicts frequently arise when a persuasive Tier 3 macro-chiasm or an attractive Tier 5 Second Temple parallel appears to suggest a reading that clashes with Tier 1 verbal morphology or Tier 2 logical connectives. Under the concentric hierarchy, the conflict is resolved decisively in favor of the inner tiers: a macro-structural symmetry cannot erase an adversative ἀλλά or transform an indicative assertion into an imperative command, nor can an extrabiblical parallel from Qumran or Philo override the immediate cotextual selection of a Greek or Hebrew lexeme. By disciplining every higher-tier hypothesis to pass through the narrow gate of original-language syntax and cotextual logic, the Six-Pillar Method safeguards exegetical objectivity while welcoming canonical and historical enrichment.

2.1 同心圓分層：為何釋經證據必須分出權威等第

嚴謹的聖經釋經不僅在於廣泛蒐集跨學科資料，更在於當結構圖表、歷史平行文獻或神學傳統彼此衝突時，如何進行方法論上的裁決。10 為杜絕主觀臆斷，六支柱釋經法嚴格執行一套呈同心圓分佈的「六層釋經證據權威階層」。11 證據階層愈貼近作者具體的語言編碼，其語義決定力便愈高；愈向外圍的正典互涉、歷史比較與接受史視域移動，該證據便愈具啟發性（heuristic）與從屬性。

2.2 第一至二層：原文形態句法與直接上下文（Cotext）

第一層（原文文本、形態學與句法關係）構成一切詮釋的基石。在提出任何語義主張前，必須先透過文本鑑別學確立最可信的作者原初讀文。12 隨後，子句句法——主詞、動詞謂語、格位關係、介詞管轄與主從結構——為句子所能承載的意義劃定不可逾越的邊界。當代希臘文語言學（波特、范寧 Buist M. Fanning、麥凱 K. L. McKay）證明，動詞形態編碼的是「體貌」（Aspect）（作者看待行動的主觀視角：完成體／不定過去式視行動為整體；未完成體／現在與未完成式視行動為持續過程），而非機械性的時間動作類型（Aktionsart）。13 若某種解釋在第一層句法上無法成立，任何宏觀結構或文化背景皆無法挽救之。

第二層（直接文學上下文／Cotext）是詞彙多義性（Polysemy）的最終仲裁者。14 單詞在孤立狀態下往往具有寬廣的語義域，唯有在具體的「組合鏈」（syntagmatic chain）中，緊鄰上下文才會篩選並激活作者所指涉的唯一特定意義。邏輯連接詞（γάρ、οὖν、ἀλλά、διὰ τοῦτο）將相鄰命題編織為因果、推論或轉折關係，直接決定段落邏輯。15

2.3 第三至六層：宏觀結構、正典互涉、歷史處境與註釋書

第三層（宏觀結構與全書論點）將段落置於作者整全的編排意圖（scopus libri）中，例如馬太福音五次講論轉折語（καὶ ἐγένετο ὅτε ἐτέλεσεν）、創世記十一次「後代／歷代記」（תּוֹלְדֹת）標題，或保羅書信的修辭編排。然而正如福克爾曼（J. P. Fokkelman）所警誡，強加的對稱交錯結構（Chiasmus）絕不可凌駕於第一、二層的線性句法之上。16

第四層（正典互涉與底本 Vorlage）運用嚴謹判準——文本可得性、詞彙回聲密度與主題連貫性（Michael Fishbane, Richard B. Hays, Abner Chou）——追蹤聖經內部釋經，同時嚴守赫施（E. D. Hirsch）對原初「確定意義」（meaning）與正典「延展意義」（significance）的界線。17 第五層（歷史文化與第二聖殿平行文獻）重建古代近東「文化長河」（John H. Walton）或希羅／第二聖殿社會矩陣（N. T. Wright, Michael F. Bird），但仍從屬於明確的文本上下文。最後，第六層（次級學術文獻與認信傳統）僅具輔助對話功能：在更正教正統神學中，唯獨聖經是「規範諸規範而不受規範的最高規範」（norma normans non normata），註釋書與教義系統皆屬「受規範的次級規範」（norma normata）。18

2.4 實作層面之證據衝突裁決機制

在具體的研討課實作中，當一個具說服力的第三層宏觀交錯結構，或一個看似迷人的第五層第二聖殿平行文獻，與第一層動詞形態或第二層邏輯連接詞發生衝突時，同心圓階層便發揮決定性的裁決作用：宏觀對稱結構絕不能抹消轉折連詞 ἀλλά，亦不能將直述語氣曲解為命令語氣；同理，昆蘭古卷或斐羅的聖經外平行文獻亦絕不能推翻希臘文或希伯來文詞彙在緊鄰上下文中所選定的特定語義。唯有令所有外圍假設通過原文句法與上下文邏輯的窄門，六支柱方法論方能在接納正典與歷史滋養的同時，堅守釋經客觀性。

03 · ARCHITECTURE OF THE SIX PILLARS 03 · 六大釋經方法論支柱之整體架構

Integration of the Six-Pillar Exegetical Method 六大釋經方法論支柱之系統整合與協同運作

● ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLE · SYNERGISTIC SPIRAL 架構原則 · 協同運作之釋經螺旋

The Six-Pillar Exegetical Method does not treat discourse analysis, verbal aspect, intertextuality, historical comparison, fallacy auditing, and pericopal synthesis as isolated compartments; each pillar interrogates and refines the others within a self-correcting hermeneutical spiral. 六大釋經方法論支柱並非將語篇分析、動詞體貌、文本互涉、歷史比較、謬誤審核與段落綜合割裂為彼此孤立的隔間；相反，六大支柱在自我修正的詮釋螺旋中彼此詰問、相互校準。

3.1 Operationalizing the Six Pillars Across the Evidentiary Hierarchy

To translate the theoretical hierarchy of Tiers 1–6 into a repeatable seminary research discipline, Part II of this compendium organizes exegesis into six interlocking methodological pillars.19 While each pillar commands its own technical apparatus, their sequence and mutual accountability are governed by the evidentiary hierarchy established in Section 02: syntactic and cotextual constraints remain sovereign over macro-structural, intertextual, and historical reconstructions.20 Rather than treating exegesis as a linear assembly line where each stage is abandoned once completed, the Six-Pillar Method operates as a self-correcting hermeneutical spiral in which macro-discourse hypotheses are continuously tested against clausal syntax and lexical semantics. This disciplined integration bridges the perennial divide between technical philology and theological synthesis, ensuring that postgraduate researchers neither lose sight of the forest for the grammatical trees nor float above the primary text in ungrounded speculation.

3.2 Pillars I–III: Literary Architecture, Morpho-Syntax, and Canonical Vorlage

Pillar I (Macro-Literary and Discourse Analysis) maps how a pericope functions within its surrounding narrative or epistolary argument. Drawing on Jeffrey T. Reed's discourse-analytical framework and Shimon Bar-Efrat's poetics of biblical narrative, Pillar I tracks participant tracking, prominence (backgrounded vs. foregrounded clauses), boundary markers (such as inclusio, chiasm, and transitional summaries), and macro-genre conventions.21 By locating the pericope within the book's overarching rhetorical dispositio, Pillar I prevents readers from misinterpreting an illustrative parenthesis as the main thesis.

Pillar II (Original-Language Morpho-Syntax and Verbal Aspect) anchors interpretation in Tier 1 linguistic reality. By diagramming independent kernel clauses against subordinate modifiers and distinguishing verbal aspect (perfective, imperfective, stative) from temporal reference, Pillar II prevents theological inferences from being built on non-existent grammatical distinctions.22 It further traces logical conjunctions, prepositional semantics, and genitive/construct relationships within the immediate cotext.

Pillar III (Canonical Intertextuality and Vorlage Criticism) investigates how biblical authors appropriate antecedent Scripture. Whether analyzing Hebrew inner-biblical exegesis or New Testament citations of the Septuagint (LXX) versus the Masoretic Text (MT), Pillar III applies Hays's sevenfold echo tests—availability, volume, recurrence, thematic coherence, historical plausibility, history of interpretation, and satisfaction—to distinguish intentional authorial allusion from coincidental vocabulary.23

3.3 Pillars IV–VI: Historical Matrix, Fallacy Audit, and Pericopal Synthesis

Pillar IV (Historical-Comparative and Second Temple Context) reconstructs the social, political, and religious world behind the text—from Ancient Near Eastern covenant treaties to Second Temple messianic texts (1 Enoch, Psalms of Solomon, Qumran)—while rigorously enforcing the distinction between shared cultural context and genetic literary dependence.24 By situating idioms, honor-shame dynamics, and institutional realia within their lived Mediterranean or Ancient Near Eastern milieu, Pillar IV prevents modern readers from importing twenty-first-century Western assumptions into ancient covenantal discourse.

Pillar V (Epistemic and Exegetical Fallacy Audit) acts as a mandatory quality-control gate before conclusions are finalized. Every lexical, grammatical, logical, and comparative claim generated in Pillars I–IV is audited against the taxonomies of James Barr, Moisés Silva, and D. A. Carson.25 Finally, Pillar VI (Pericopal Synthesis and Theological Proclamation) integrates locutionary content, illocutionary force, and perlocutionary intent (J. L. Austin, Kevin J. Vanhoozer) into a single, text-governed theological thesis.26 Rather than jumping prematurely from a lexical token to systematic dogma, Pillar VI ensures that ecclesial proclamation remains tethered to the pericope's concrete communicative act and canonical trajectory.27

3.4 Spiral Feedback Loops Between the Six Pillars

Crucially, the Six-Pillar Method is not a one-way funnel in which earlier pillars are discarded once later pillars are engaged. When Pillar III uncovers an unexpected Septuagintal Vorlage or inner-biblical echo, that discovery prompts a fresh return to Pillar I to re-evaluate whether a structural boundary or narrative motif was intentionally shaped by the antecedent text. Likewise, when Pillar IV clarifies an ancient honor-shame convention or imperial patronage structure, the exegete cycles back to Pillar II to verify whether the Greek or Hebrew syntax actually encodes that social dynamic. Finally, Pillar V audits the entire feedback loop, pruning speculative bridges before Pillar VI formulates the pericopal thesis. This iterative discipline ensures that creativity in historical and canonical synthesis never outruns philological accountability. In graduate research seminars, students are therefore trained to document their work in an auditable dossier that records every textual variant, syntactic diagram, intertextual criterion, historical primary source, and rejected interpretive option before drafting the final theological exposition, thereby rendering every exegetical judgment transparent and falsifiable to the wider scholarly guild.

為了將第一至六層的證據權威理論轉化為神學研究院可重複操作的學術訓練，本典籍第二部將綜合釋經法組織為「六大相互扣連的方法論支柱」。19 儘管每一支柱皆擁有其專屬的技術工具與學術文獻，但它們的執行序位與相互問責機制，始終受制於第二節所確立的證據階層：原文句法與直接上下文對宏觀結構、正典互涉與歷史重建擁有絕對的規範權。20

3.2 第一至三支柱：宏觀語篇、形態句法與正典底本

第一支柱（宏觀文學與語篇分析）描繪經文段落在全書敘事或書信論證中的功能定位。借鏡里德（Jeffrey T. Reed）的語篇分析框架與巴爾－埃夫拉特（Shimon Bar-Efrat）的聖經敘事詩學，第一支柱追蹤參與者線索、前景突出機制（Prominence）、邊界標記（如首尾呼應 inclusio 與轉折摘要）以及文體規範。21

第二支柱（原文形態句法與動詞體貌）將詮釋牢牢錨定於第一層語言事實。透過圖解獨立核心子句與從屬修飾語的關係，並嚴格區分動詞體貌（完成體、未完成體、狀態體）與時間指涉，第二支柱防止神學推論建立在並不存在的語法區分之上。22

第三支柱（正典文本互涉與底本 Vorlage 鑑別）考察聖經作者如何援引並詮釋先前啟示。無論是剖析希伯來聖經內部的釋經傳統，抑或比較新約引文與《七十士譯本》（LXX）及馬所拉文本（MT）之異同，第三支柱皆採用海斯（Richard B. Hays）的七項回聲判準——尤重可得性、回聲密度、重複出現率與主題連貫性——以區辨作者有意之正典互涉與偶發詞彙重疊。23

3.3 第四至六支柱：歷史矩陣、謬誤審核與段落綜合

第四支柱（歷史比較與第二聖殿處境）重建文本背後的社會、政治與宗教實況——從古代近東宗主條約至第二聖殿彌賽亞文獻（《以諾一書》、《所羅門詩篇》、昆蘭古卷）——同時嚴格區分共享的文化「處境」與直接的文學「依賴」。24

第五支柱（認識論與釋經謬誤審核）在結論定案前擔任強制性的品質把關閘門。第一至四支柱所產出的每一項詞彙、語法、邏輯與比較主張，皆須通過巴爾（James Barr）、席爾瓦（Moisés Silva）與卡森（D. A. Carson）所建構的謬誤分類表之檢驗。25 最後，第六支柱（段落綜合與神學宣講）整合「言說內容」（locution）、「言內行動力」（illocution）與「言後果效意圖」（perlocution，參 Austin 與 Vanhoozer），凝練出一項受文本嚴格規範的神學核心命題。26 第六支柱杜絕從單一詞彙跳躍至系統教義的斷章取義，確保教會講道始終緊扣經文具體的溝通行動。27

3.4 六大支柱間之螺旋回饋機制

關鍵在於，六支柱方法論並非一條完成前站便棄置不顧的單向流水線。當第三支柱發掘出意料之外的七十士譯本底本或聖經內部回聲時，該發現會促使釋經者折返第一支柱，重新評估結構邊界或敘事母題是否受先前經文刻意塑造；同理，當第四支柱澄清某項古代榮辱規範或帝國庇護關係時，研究者便須回溯第二支柱，檢驗希臘文或希伯來文句法是否確實編碼了該社會動態。最終，第五支柱對整條回饋迴路進行總體審核，修剪缺乏文本根據的臆測橋樑，方才交由第六支柱凝練段落神學命題。

04 · EPISTEMIC CONTROLS & FALLACY AUDIT 04 · 認識論控管與釋經謬誤審核

Epistemic Controls and Fallacy Auditing (Barr, Silva, Carson) 認識論控管與釋經謬誤審核：詞彙、語法、邏輯與平行狂熱

● FALLACY AUDIT RULE · SYNCHRONIC PRIORITY 謬誤審核法則 · 共時語義優先律

Words do not carry their etymologies or entire lexical histories into every sentence. Meaning is generated synchronically at the level of the clause and immediate cotext, never by assembling lexical atoms or importing unverified extrabiblical parallels. 詞彙並不會將其詞源歷史或全部詞典義項同時帶入每一個句子之中。意義是在子句與直接上下文的共時層面上生成的，絕非拼湊詞素或強行嫁接未經檢驗的聖經外平行文獻。

4.1 Lexical and Semantic Fallacies: Barr, Silva, and Carson

A primary purpose of the six-tier evidentiary hierarchy is to function as an epistemic filter against recurring interpretive errors.28 Building upon James Barr's landmark critique in The Semantics of Biblical Language (1961), Moisés Silva's Biblical Words and Their Meaning (1994), and D. A. Carson's Exegetical Fallacies (1996), the Six-Pillar Method mandates systematic auditing across four fallacy domains.29 Without such explicit methodological policing, even scholars fluent in biblical languages routinely smuggle theological preconceptions into isolated lexical tokens, treat diachronic word histories as synchronic definitions, or mistake surface-level literary parallels for genetic dependence. Epistemic vigilance is therefore not a peripheral appendix to exegesis; it is the indispensable immune system of sound biblical interpretation.

First, lexical and semantic fallacies arise when interpreters treat words as autonomous containers of theological concepts rather than contextually conditioned signs:

  • The Root Fallacy: Assuming a word's synchronic meaning is governed by its diachronic etymology—such as claiming ὑπηρέτης means "under-rower" (ὑπό + ἐρέσσω) rather than "attendant/officer," claiming ἀπόστολος means "one sent" solely by decomposing ἀπό + στέλλω, or insisting μονογενής denotes "only-begotten" (from γεννάω) rather than "unique, one-of-a-kind" (from γένος).30
  • Illegitimate Totality Transfer (Barr): Importing the entire semantic range of a lexeme (or every theological nuance across the canon) into a single pericopal occurrence, such as loading all Pauline uses of ἐκκλησία, σάρξ, or νόμος into one local clause regardless of its immediate syntagmatic constraints.31
  • Semantic Anachronism and Expanded Semantic Fields: Retrojecting modern meanings onto ancient tokens (e.g., equating Paul's δύναμις in Rom 1:16 with nineteenth-century "dynamite"), appealing to obscure lexical entries to evade a plain-sense reading, or forcing rigid technical distinctions onto stylistic synonyms (e.g., ἀγαπάω vs. φιλέω in John 21:15–17).32

4.2 Grammatical and Logical Fallacies

Second, grammatical fallacies occur when morphology is divorced from aspectual linguistics and clausal pragmatics. The most pervasive error is the punctiliar aorist fallacy—claiming that an aorist tense-form proves a "once-for-all" historical event, thereby confusing perfective verbal aspect (viewing an action as a complete whole) with objective Aktionsart.33 Equally hazardous are exaggerated middle-voice claims (forcing reflexive agency onto standard Hellenistic deponent verbs), treating morphological labels (such as genitive subtypes or conditional classes) as self-executing rather than pragmatically determined, and confusing grammatical semantics with discourse pragmatics.34

Third, logical fallacies distort inferential reasoning through false disjunctions (forcing "either-or" choices between complementary realities such as history vs. theology, literal vs. figurative sense, or divine sovereignty vs. human responsibility), selective prejudicing of evidence (marshaling only supportive texts while ignoring counter-evidence), and illegitimate negative inferences (assuming the converse of a true proposition must likewise hold).35 In biblical argumentation, such logical shortcuts often masquerade as doctrinal rigor while silently flattening the nuanced syntax of the biblical writers. Careful attention to concessive, causal, and conditional clauses protects the interpreter from imposing binary Western syllogisms onto Hebraic and Hellenistic discourse.

4.3 Historical Fallacies: Parallelomania vs. Cultural Context

Fourth, historical and presuppositional fallacies surface when Tier 5 comparative data override Tiers 1–2 syntax or when an external dogmatic system silences the text's particular historical voice. Samuel Sandmel coined "parallelomania" to diagnose the tendency to posit direct literary dependence between biblical texts and Dead Sea Scrolls, Philo, rabbinic traditions, or Greco-Roman treatises based on superficial verbal resemblances.36 Following Troels Engberg-Pedersen, responsible exegesis treats ancient comparative literature as a shared cultural context—an active social and conceptual environment in which biblical authors participated—that illuminates unstated presuppositions without displacing the biblical author's distinctive communicative intent.

4.4 Institutionalizing the Fallacy Audit in Exegetical Writing

To operationalize Pillar V in graduate research papers, every exegetical draft should undergo an explicit pre-submission fallacy checklist before any theological synthesis is finalized. First, the researcher must verify that no lexical claim relies on diachronic etymology or imports multiple dictionary senses simultaneously (Barr's totality transfer). Second, every aspectual claim regarding Greek or Hebrew verbs must be checked to confirm that perfective/imperfective viewpoint has not been conflated with temporal Aktionsart. Third, all logical transitions must be tested for excluded-middle dichotomies and selective evidence. Fourth, every Second Temple or Greco-Roman parallel must be accompanied by an explicit control demonstrating chronological availability and contextual relevance rather than mere verbal similarity. Only arguments that survive this fourfold audit merit inclusion in the final pericopal synthesis. By embedding fallacy detection directly into the exegetical architecture rather than treating it as an optional postscript, the Six-Pillar Method cultivates intellectual honesty, guards against homiletical sensationalism, and trains interpreters to distinguish sharply between what a text demonstrably asserts and what a preacher merely wishes it to say.

4.1 詞彙與語義謬誤：巴爾、席爾瓦與卡森的批判

六層證據權威階層的核心功能之一，在於充當認識論過濾器，系統性攔截反覆出現的釋經錯誤。28 承續詹姆斯·巴爾（James Barr）《聖經語言的語義學》（1961）、莫伊塞斯·席爾瓦（Moisés Silva）《聖經詞彙與其意義》（1994）以及卡森（D. A. Carson）《釋經謬誤》（1996）的奠基性批判，六支柱釋經法規定所有詮釋結論必須通過四大謬誤領域的強制審核。29

首先，當解經者將詞彙視為獨立承載神學概念的原子，而非受上下文制約的語言符號時，便會產生詞彙與語義謬誤：

  • 詞根謬誤（Root Fallacy）： 假定詞彙的共時意義受其歷時詞源支配——例如主張 ὑπηρέτης 意為「底層划槳手」（ὑπό + ἐρέσσω）而非「侍從／官員」，或堅稱 μονογενής 必源自 γεννάω（「獨生」）而非 γένος（「獨一無二」）。30
  • 非法總體轉移（Illegitimate Totality Transfer）： 巴爾所命名的此項謬誤，指將某詞在全本正典或整個語料庫中的所有語義面向，同時強行塞入單一經文段落中（例如將保羅書信中 ἐκκλησία 或 νόμος 的全部神學內涵一股腦讀入單一子句）。31
  • 語義時代錯置與擴張語義場： 將現代意義逆向投射至古代詞彙（如將羅 1:16 之 δύναμις 曲解為十九世紀發明的「炸藥」），或在修辭同義詞之間強加僵化的神學區分（如約 21:15–17 之 ἀγαπάω 與 φιλέω）。32

4.2 語法與邏輯謬誤

其次，當形態學脫離體貌語言學與子句語用學時，便會出現語法謬誤。其中最普遍的是「瞬間不定過去式謬誤」（Punctiliar Aorist Fallacy）——宣稱不定過去式動詞必然證明「一次性的、瞬間完成的」歷史行動，混淆了主觀的完成體體貌（視行動為整體）與客觀的動作類型（Aktionsart）。33 同樣危險的還有過度詮釋關身語態（對希臘化時期常見的異態動詞強加反身含義），以及將所有格分類標籤視為自動生效而忽略上下文語用。34

第三，邏輯謬誤藉由「虛假選言」（False Disjunctions）扭曲推論——在互補的真理之間製造非此即彼的對立（如歷史與神學、神的主權與人的責任），並伴隨選擇性偏袒證據與非法否定推論。35

4.3 歷史謬誤：平行狂熱與文化處境之辨

第四，當第五層比較資料越權推翻第一、二層句法時，便會產生歷史與預設謬誤。桑德梅爾（Samuel Sandmel）以「平行狂熱」（Parallelomania）診斷學者僅憑表面詞彙相似，便斷言聖經作者直接依賴死海古卷、斐羅或希羅哲學論著。36 依循恩貝里－佩德森（Troels Engberg-Pedersen）的洞見，嚴謹釋經將古代比較文獻視為共享的文化「處境」，用以闡明未明言的預設，而非取代聖經作者獨特的神學聲音。

4.4 將謬誤審核制度化於釋經寫作中

為使第五支柱在神學研究院論文寫作中具備可操作性，每一份釋經初稿在定稿神學綜合前，皆須接受明確的四重審核清單：第一，核查所有詞彙主張是否誤用歷時詞源或陷入巴爾所批判的非法總體轉移；第二，檢驗所有希臘文或希伯來文動詞體貌論證，確認未將完成體／未完成體視角混同於時間動作類型；第三，審查所有邏輯推論是否暗藏排中律二分法或選擇性取證；第四，為每一項第二聖殿或希羅平行文獻提出時代可得性與語境相關性之明證，而非僅憑表面字句雷同。唯有通過此四重過濾的論點，方能進入最終的段落綜合。

05 · BENCHMARK MODEL: MATT 9:35–38 05 · 基準釋經模型：馬太福音九章35–38節

The Benchmark Model: Multi-Pillar Exegesis of Matthew 9:35–38 基準釋經示範：馬太福音九章35–38節之六支柱整合解析

● BENCHMARK PERICOPE · MATTHEW 9:35–38 基準示範經文 · 馬太福音九章35–38節

Matthew 9:35–38 serves as the Compendium's benchmark pericope because all six methodological pillars converge visibly within four compact verses: inclusio with 4:23, aspectual contrast between imperfective itinerancy and aorist compassion, LXX/MT shepherd-harvest intertextuality, Second Temple messianic expectations, fallacy auditing, and missional synthesis. 馬太福音九章35–38節作為本典籍的基準示範經文，因短短四節內清晰交匯了全部六大釋經支柱：與四章23節的首尾呼應、未完成體巡迴與不定過去式憐憫之體貌對比、LXX/MT 牧人與莊稼互涉、第二聖殿彌賽亞期望、謬誤審核與宣教神學綜合。

5.1 Pillar I & Pillar II: Macro-Literary Inclusio and Greek Verbal Aspect

In Pillar I (Macro-Literary and Discourse Analysis), Matt 9:35–38 functions simultaneously as a closing structural inclusio and a forward-pointing narrative hinge.37 Verse 35 reproduces Matt 4:23 almost verbatim—περιῆγεν... διδάσκων ἐν ταῖς συναγωγαῖς αὐτῶν καὶ κηρύσσων τὸ εὐαγγέλιον τῆς βασιλείας καὶ θεραπεύων πᾶσαν νόσον καὶ πᾶσαν μαλακίαν—framing the Messiah's authoritative word (Sermon on the Mount, chs. 5–7) and messianic deeds (Three Miracle Cycles, chs. 8–9) into a unified diptych.38 Simultaneously, vv. 36–38 pivot the narrative from Jesus's solitary ministry to the commissioning of the Twelve in the Missionary Discourse (Matt 10:1–42), demonstrating how Matthean narrative summaries routinely bind major compositional blocks together.

In Pillar II (Morpho-Syntax and Verbal Aspect), Matthew's verbal architecture establishes precise discourse prominence.39 Verse 35 opens with the imperfective περιῆγεν ("he was going about"), presenting Jesus's Galilean tour as an ongoing, iterative background summary, elaborated by three adverbial present participles of manner: διδάσκων (teaching), κηρύσσων (proclaiming), and θεραπεύων (healing). In v. 36, the aspect shifts to the aorist mainline verb ἐσπλαγχνίσθη ("he had compassion"), foregrounding Jesus's decisive messianic response upon seeing (ἰδών) the crowds. The causal clause ὅτι ἦσαν ἐσκυλμένοι καὶ ἐρριμμένοι deploys two periphrastic/adjectival perfect passive participles—ἐσκυλμένοι ("harassed/flayed") and ἐρριμμένοι ("cast down/helpless")—depicting the enduring, prostrate condition of Israel under negligent leadership.40 In v. 38, the prayer imperative δεήθητε (aorist) directs disciples to petition the Lord of the harvest that he might "thrust out" (ἐκβάλῃ, aorist subjunctive) workers into his harvest.

5.2 Pillar III & Pillar IV: Old Testament Vorlage and Second Temple Matrix

In Pillar III (Intertextuality and Vorlage), the simile ὡσεὶ πρόβατα μὴ ἔχοντα ποιμένα (v. 36) activates a dense prophetic chain: Moses's prayer for a successor in Num 27:17 (LXX ὡσεὶ πρόβατα οἷς οὐκ ἔστιν ποιμήν), Micaiah's judgment oracle in 1 Kgs 22:17, Ezekiel's indictment of Israel's predatory shepherds in Ezek 34:5–6, and Zech 10:2.41 By echoing Moses's petition for a shepherd who would lead Israel out and bring them in, Matthew presents Jesus not merely as a compassionate healer, but as YHWH's promised Davidic shepherd-king who steps into the leadership vacuum left by Israel's negligent authorities. Likewise, the eschatological harvest metaphor (vv. 37–38, ὁ μὲν θερισμὸς πολύς, οἱ δὲ ἐργάται ὀλίγοι) resonates with Isa 9:3 (joy at the harvest) and Joel 3:13 [MT 4:13], signaling that Israel's restoration—simultaneously an ingathering of the vulnerable and a crisis of eschatological urgency—has dawned in Jesus's ministry.42

In Pillar IV (Historical-Comparative Context), first-century Galilean villages (πόλεις πάσας καὶ τὰς κώμας) suffered heavy Herodian and Roman taxation alongside economic displacement and scribal neglect. Second Temple witnesses—especially Psalms of Solomon 17:21–40 and Qumran's Damascus Document (CD 13:7–9)—document widespread longing for a Davidic shepherd-king who would gather the scattered flock, purify the land, and judge corrupt leaders.43

5.3 Pillar V & Pillar VI: Fallacy Audit and Pericopal Synthesis

Applying Pillar V (Fallacy Audit) protects the text from three common distortions: (1) over-etymologizing ἐσπλαγχνίσθη via visceral "bowels" (σπλάγχνα) rather than its synchronic Koine sense of deep covenantal compassion; (2) imposing punctiliar Aktionsart onto ἐσπλαγχνίσθη or ἐκβάλῃ as though the aorist proved a single instantaneous emotion rather than perfective mainline prominence; and (3) committing illegitimate totality transfer on βασιλεία by importing every canonical nuance of the kingdom into the summary phrase τὸ εὐαγγέλιον τῆς βασιλείας.44 Finally, Pillar VI (Pericopal Synthesis) distills the pericope's unified illocutionary claim: The Davidic Messiah's compassionate shepherd-ministry—enacted through teaching, kingdom proclamation, and healing—summons his disciples to pray for and participate in God's urgent eschatological harvest.45

5.4 Why Matthew 9:35–38 Models Evidentiary Discipline

Viewed as a pedagogical benchmark, Matthew 9:35–38 demonstrates how the six tiers of evidence reinforce one another without confusion of authority. Tier 1 verbal aspect distinguishes the backgrounded imperfective summary of Jesus's itinerancy (περιῆγεν) from the foregrounded aorist of messianic compassion (ἐσπλαγχνίσθη), while Tier 2 cotextual logic links that compassion directly to the disciples' mandate to pray (δεήθητε οὖν) in v. 38. Tier 3 macro-structure confirms that this summary intentionally closes the chs. 4:23–9:35 inclusio while launching the ch. 10 mission. Tier 4 intertextuality and Tier 5 Second Temple shepherd expectations then deepen the theological resonance of the shepherdless flock without ever displacing the primary Greek syntax. Consequently, the exegete can verify every theological claim—from Jesus's identity as the promised Davidic shepherd to the eschatological urgency of apostolic mission—through transparent, falsifiable linguistic and canonical evidence rather than devotional conjecture, establishing a reproducible standard for all subsequent pericopal studies in Part II.

5.2 第三與第四支柱：舊約底本互涉與第二聖殿處境

在第三支柱（正典互涉與底本）中，明喻 ὡσεὶ πρόβατα μὴ ἔχοντα ποιμένα（36節）激活了一條綿密的先知傳統鏈：摩西在民數記廿七章17節求立繼承者的禱告（LXX: ὡσεὶ πρόβατα οἷς οὐκ ἔστιν ποιμήν）、米該雅在列王紀上廿二章17節的審判神諭、以西結書卅四章5–6節對失職牧人的控訴，以及撒迦利亞書十章2節。41 同樣地，末世莊稼隱喻（37–38節，ὁ μὲν θερισμὸς πολύς）遙應以賽亞書九章3節與約珥書三章13節（MT 4:13），宣告以色列的復興已在耶穌的事奉中破曉。42

在第四支柱（歷史比較處境）中，第一世紀加利利城鄉（πόλεις πάσας καὶ τὰς κώμας）承受希律政權與羅馬重稅及宗教領袖的忽視。第二聖殿文獻——尤以《所羅門詩篇》十七章21–40節與昆蘭《大馬士革約書》（CD 13:7–9）為著——見證了當時猶太群體對大衛牧者君王招聚散羊的深切盼望。43

5.3 第五與第六支柱：謬誤審核與段落神學綜合

執行第五支柱（謬誤審核）能防範三項常見偏差：(1) 過度詞源化 ἐσπλαγχνίσθη 為生理「臟腑翻騰」，忽略其共時聖約憐憫義；(2) 將瞬間動作類型強加於 ἐσπλαγχνίσθη 或 ἐκβάλῃ；(3) 對 βασιλεία 犯下非法總體轉移。44 最後，第六支柱（段落綜合）凝練出本段經文的整全言內行動主張：大衛彌賽亞透過教導、天國宣講與醫治所施行的憐憫牧養事奉，呼召門徒為上帝迫切的末世莊稼禱告並投身其中。45

5.4 為何馬太福音九章35–38節堪稱證據紀律之典範

作為教學基準模型，馬太福音九章35–38節清晰示範六層證據如何在不混淆權威的前提下彼此強化：第一層動詞體貌區分了耶穌巡迴事奉的背景未完成體（περιῆγεν）與前景突出彌賽亞憐憫的不定過去式（ἐσπλαγχνίσθη），而第二層上下文邏輯則藉由 δεήθητε οὖν 將此憐憫直接連結至第38節的禱告命令；第三層宏觀結構確認此總結既封閉了 4:23–9:35 的首尾呼應，又開啟第十章宣教差遣；第四層正典互涉與第五層第二聖殿牧者期望則在不取代原文希臘文句法的前提下，深化了「無牧之羊」的神學共鳴。

Exegesis remains incomplete until Tier 1–5 analysis culminates in Pillar VI: articulating the pericope's unified illocutionary act—what the divine-human author is doing through these words—so that canonical theology and ecclesial proclamation remain anchored in determinate textual meaning. 若第一至五層的分析未能匯聚於第六支柱，釋經便尚未完成：唯有清晰闡明經文段落的整全「言內行動」（神人作者藉這段話語所施行的行動），正典神學與教會宣講才能牢牢錨定於確定的文本意義之上。

6.1 Integrating Locution, Illocution, and Perlocution

A recurring temptation in postgraduate biblical studies is antiquarian atomism—accumulating impressive morpho-syntactic tables, text-critical apparatus notes, and Second Temple parallels without ever synthesizing what the pericope communicates as a unified theological claim.46 Conversely, systematicians and preachers sometimes bypass Tier 1–5 analysis altogether, extracting isolated dicta probantia (proof-texts) without regard for literary context. To bridge historical-grammatical description and ecclesial theology without committing proof-texting, Pillar VI draws on Speech-Act Theory as developed by J. L. Austin, John R. Searle, and Kevin J. Vanhoozer.47

Every biblical pericope operates simultaneously across three communicative dimensions: (1) the locutionary act—the concrete Hebrew, Aramaic, or Greek propositions, lexical senses, and syntactic structures established by Tiers 1–2; (2) the illocutionary act—what the divine-human author does in uttering those words (e.g., promising, warning, commissioning, lamenting, or declaring covenantal mercy); and (3) the perlocutionary intent—the transformative cognitive, affective, and ethical response sought from the listening community.48 By distinguishing these three dimensions, Pillar VI prevents preachers and theologians from reducing narrative or poetic genres to bare propositional abstractions, while simultaneously anchoring ethical application in the text's determinate linguistic action. Authentic theological synthesis thus honors both the divine authority of the canon and the literary particularity of each biblical witness.

6.2 Navigating Meaning, Significance, and Canonical Trajectories

In moving from pericopal synthesis to biblical and systematic theology, the Six-Pillar Method enforces E. D. Hirsch Jr.'s vital distinction between determinate textual meaning (fixed by the author's historical-linguistic act) and unfolding significance (the text's relationship to subsequent canonical revelation, redemptive-historical fulfillment, and contemporary contexts).49 While significance expands as a passage is read within the whole canon of Old and New Testaments (Robert W. Wall, Brevard S. Childs), later doctrinal formulations must never be retrojected anachronistically to erase the particular historical voice of the earlier testament. Preserving this boundary allows biblical theology to trace genuine typological escalation and promise-fulfillment trajectories across covenants without violating the grammatical-historical integrity of the original witness.50

Here Walter C. Kaiser Jr.'s "principlizing" model and Grant R. Osborne's "contextualization" helix converge: the exegete abstracts the enduring theological principle embedded in the text's illocutionary force, tests that principle against the analogy of antecedent Scripture, and re-applies it to analogous contemporary situations without violating the text's original genre or covenantal horizon.51 By maintaining this disciplined bridge between ancient locution and contemporary perlocution, biblical theology avoids both sterile historicism—which locks Scripture in the past—and subjective allegorizing—which detaches application from textual warrant. Every legitimate sermonic or doctrinal claim remains accountable to the grammatical, literary, and canonical structures uncovered in Pillars I through V.

6.3 Roadmap to the Six Dedicated Pillar Monographs of Part II

Having established the architectural blueprint, evidentiary hierarchy, fallacy-auditing filters, and Matthew 9:35–38 benchmark model, Part II now dedicates six specialized reference pages to each methodological pillar.52 Students and researchers should consult these monographs sequentially when executing a full exegetical paper, returning iteratively along the hermeneutical spiral whenever new syntactic, intertextual, or historical evidence challenges an earlier hypothesis.53 Ultimately, disciplined exegesis serves the Church as an act of intellectual love—hearing the Word of God with maximal philological rigor, epistemic humility, and theological fidelity.54

6.4 From Academic Exegesis to Ecclesial Transformation

When practiced with methodological integrity, the Six-Pillar Method dissolves the false dichotomy between rigorous academic scholarship and vital pastoral ministry. Far from reducing Scripture to a lifeless archaeological artifact, Tier 1–5 precision clears away eisegesis and anachronism so that the authentic illocutionary voice of the biblical text can address the contemporary church with undiminished clarity. Whether in a doctoral dissertation, a seminary exegesis seminar, or weekly pulpit exposition, the interpreter's mandate remains identical: to submit every presupposition to the bar of the original text, to trace its canonical resonance with sobriety, and to proclaim its determinate meaning for the renewal of faith, hope, and obedience. In this way, the architecture of Part II equips the interpreter not merely to analyze ancient manuscripts, but to stand faithfully under the living address of the triune God who speaks through Scripture to form a discerning, missionally obedient covenant community across every cultural horizon.

6.1 整合言說內容、言內行動與言後果效

神學研究院聖經研究常面臨一項誘惑，即「考古式原子主義」（antiquarian atomism）——累積了可觀的形態句法表、文本鑑別註腳與第二聖殿平行文獻，卻始終未能將段落整全的神學主張綜合表達出來。46 為了在歷史語法描述與教會神學之間架起橋樑，同時杜絕斷章取義的「證明經文」（proof-texting），第六支柱援引奧斯汀（J. L. Austin）、塞爾（John R. Searle）與范胡澤（Kevin J. Vanhoozer）所發展的言語行動理論（Speech-Act Theory）。47

每一段聖經經文皆同時運作於三個溝通維度之上：(1) 言說行動（Locutionary Act）——由第一、二層所確立的希伯來文、亞蘭文或希臘文具體命題；(2) 言內行動（Illocutionary Act）——作者在說出這些話語時所施行的行動（如應許、警告、差遣、哀歌或宣告聖約憐憫）；(3) 言後果效意圖（Perlocutionary Intent）——期待聽眾群體產生的認知、情感與倫理轉化回應。48

6.2 駕馭確定意義、延展意義與正典軌跡

在從段落釋經邁向聖經神學與系統神學的過程中，六支柱方法論嚴守赫施（E. D. Hirsch Jr.）對文本「確定意義」（Meaning）（由作者歷史語言行動所固定）與「延展意義」（Significance）（文本與後續正典啟示及當代處境的關聯）之關鍵區分。49 儘管當經文置於新舊約整全正典中閱讀時其神學意義會不斷深化（Robert W. Wall, Brevard S. Childs），但後世的教義系統絕不可時代錯置地逆向抹平早期見證的獨特歷史聲音。50

在此，凱澤的「原則化」（principlizing）模型與奧斯本的「處境化」（contextualization）螺旋相互匯合：釋經者從文本言內行動力中提煉出恆久的神學原則，以先前啟示的類比加以檢驗，並在不違背原文文體與聖約視域的前提下，將之應用於類比的當代處境。51

6.3 第二部六大支柱專論之導覽藍圖

在確立了整體架構藍圖、六層證據權威階層、謬誤審核過濾器以及馬太福音九章35–38節之基準模型後，本典籍第二部隨後將為每一項釋經支柱獻上一篇專門的方法論專論。52 研究者在撰寫完整釋經論文時，應依序研讀這六篇專論，每當新的句法或文本互涉證據挑戰既有假設時，便沿著詮釋學螺旋反覆校準。53 歸根究柢，嚴謹的釋經是服事教會的理性愛心行動——以最高規格的語文學精準度與神學忠誠，聆聽上帝的話語。54

6.4 從學術釋經邁向教會群體之生命更新

當釋經者以方法論的誠信操練六支柱模型時，嚴謹學術研究與活潑牧養事奉之間的虛假對立便隨之消融。第一至五層的精準分析絕非將聖經化約為毫無生氣的考古標本，而是清除私意強解與時代錯置的雜質，使聖經文本真實的言內行動聲音得以清晰無阻地向當代教會發話。無論是在博士論文、神學院釋經研討課，抑或主日講壇的經文宣講中，詮釋者的使命始終如一：將一切先入為主的預設交由原文文本審判，以清醒節制的態度追蹤正典共鳴，並忠實宣講經文確定的救贖意義，以造就信徒的信心、盼望與順服。
