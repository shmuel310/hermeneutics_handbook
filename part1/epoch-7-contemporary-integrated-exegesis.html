01 · THEOLOGICAL INTERPRETATION (TIS) 01 · 聖經的神學詮釋（TIS）

The Theological Interpretation of Scripture: Dogmatic Ontology, Ecclesial Retrieval, and the Regula Fidei 聖經的神學詮釋（TIS）：教義本體論、教會傳統取回與信仰準則

● EPOCHAL AXIOM · THEOLOGICAL ONTOLOGY OF SCRIPTURE 時代公理 · 聖經的神學本體論

The Theological Interpretation of Scripture (TIS) heals the Enlightenment divide between historical criticism and Christian dogmatics by locating biblical texts within the Trinitarian economy of grace, where the Church reads as creatura Verbi under the canonical governance of the regula fidei. 「聖經的神學詮釋」（TIS）透過將聖經文本重置於三一上帝恩典經世（Trinitarian economy of grace）之中，彌合了啟蒙運動以來歷史批判學與教義神學的裂痕；在此視域下，教會作為「聖言的受造物」（creatura Verbi），在正典「信仰準則」（regula fidei）的引導下進行順服的閱讀。

1.1 Beyond Historicist Fragmentation: The Emergence of Theological Interpretation

By the closing decades of the twentieth century, biblical scholarship faced a widely diagnosed epistemological crisis: the professionalization of the historical-critical method had severed rigorous philological exegesis from the doctrinal life of the church.01 Treating Scripture merely as an ancient Near Eastern or Greco-Roman artifact dissected by ostensibly neutral reason produced an antiquarian atomism incapable of articulating how ancient texts function as the living address of the Triune God. The Theological Interpretation of Scripture (TIS) emerged in the 1990s and early 2000s not as a single rigid method, but as a convergent ecclesial posture dedicated to recovering the Bible's primary theological identity as Sacra Scriptura.02

At the ontological root of TIS stands the work of British systematic theologian John Webster (1955–2016). In Holy Scripture: A Dogmatic Sketch (2003) and The Domain of the Word (2012), Webster argued that hermeneutics must be preceded by a theological ontology of the text.03 Scripture cannot be adequately theorized through general anthropology or secular textuality alone, because it exists within the Trinitarian economy of self-communication. In divine aseity, God sanctifies, preserves, and inspirits creaturely human words so that they mediate Christ's living presence. Consequently, the church is fundamentally creatura Verbi ("a creature of the Word"): its interpretive acts possess legitimacy only insofar as they are Spirit-dependent modes of attentive listening to prior divine speech.04

1.2 Communicative Theodrama, Precritical Retrieval, and Readerly Virtues

Within this ontological horizon, leading TIS theorists have advanced complementary proposals for how historical exegesis and dogmatic theology co-inhere:

  • Kevin J. Vanhoozer (Canonical-Linguistic Theodrama): Across First Theology (2002), The Drama of Doctrine (2005), and Biblical Authority after Babel (2016), Vanhoozer replaced a Pelagian "hermeneutics of glory" with a "hermeneutics of the cross" grounded in Trinitarian communicative action: the Father is the transcendent Locator, the Son is the definitive divine Illocution, and the Holy Spirit is the efficacious Perlocutor who enables faithful ecclesial performance of the scriptural script.05
  • Daniel J. Treier (Catalytic Retrieval): In Introducing Theological Interpretation of Scripture (2008) and Theology and the Mirror of Scripture (2015, co-authored with Vanhoozer), Treier framed TIS as an ecumenical catalyst marked by three impulses: (1) discerning retrieval of precritical patristic and medieval figural reading, (2) normative engagement with the ecumenical creeds, and (3) explicit reliance on the Spirit's illumination across global reception history.06
  • Stephen E. Fowl (Readerly Virtues & Thomistic Polyphony): In Engaging Scripture (1998) and Theological Interpretation of Scripture (2009), Fowl argued that the telos of reading is not neutral historical reconstruction but deepening communion with God and neighbor. Drawing on Thomas Aquinas (Summa Theologiae I, q. 1, a. 10), Fowl contended that the literal sense (sensus literalis) possesses a "bounded plurality" or multi-voiced richness authored by God, requiring readerly virtues of humility, charity, and repentance.07
  • J. Todd Billings (Covenantal Participation): In The Word of God for the People of God (2010), Billings articulated a Reformed TIS model rooted in union with Christ. Rather than pitting sola Scriptura against tradition or discarding grammatical-historical tools, Billings embeds lexical and historical rigor within covenantal participation and confessional accountability.08

1.3 The Patristic Regula Fidei as Canonical Grammar

A decisive unifying thread across TIS is the recovery of the patristic Rule of Faith (regula fidei). As Robert W. Wall and Kevin Vanhoozer observe, Irenaeus of Lyons (Adversus haereses 1.8.1; 3.4.1–2) and Tertullian (De praescriptione haereticorum 9.1) did not treat the Rule of Faith as an alien ecclesiastical imposition upon Scripture, but as an internal apostolic distillation of Scripture's master Christological storyline.09 Using Irenaeus's famous mosaic analogy, heretics rearrange the golden tesserae of biblical texts to depict a fox or a dog, whereas the regula fidei supplies the true portrait of the King. Functioning as a gubernaculum interpretationis ("hermeneutical governor"), the Rule of Faith establishes the non-negotiable Trinitarian grammar within which rigorous philological exegesis operates. Far from stifling close textual inquiry, this canonical grammar liberates the interpreter from modern naturalistic reductionism and provides a shared ecumenical horizon for reading the Old and New Testaments as one unified witness to Jesus Christ.

1.4 Critical Assessment: Guardrails Against Ecclesial Fideism

Critics of TIS—including Stanley E. Porter and D. A. Carson—have cautioned that an undisciplined appeal to "theological interpretation" can inadvertently marginalize grammatical-historical controls, collapse biblical theology into anachronistic systematic categories, or privilege later ecclesiastical tradition over the plain syntactic sense of the text. In response, the most durable formulations of TIS (particularly those of Vanhoozer, Treier, and Billings) reject any false dichotomy between philology and dogmatics. Rather than replacing Hebrew, Aramaic, and Greek exegesis with homiletical allegory, authentic theological interpretation insists that attending to the canonical regula fidei presupposes, rather than bypasses, painstaking attention to lexical semantics, discourse grammar, and historical context. Moreover, by locating interpretation within the global communion of saints—across patristic, medieval, Reformation, and majority-world voices—TIS guards against the provincialism of modern Western academic guild consensus. In this way, TIS functions not as an escape from exegetical labor, but as its proper Trinitarian horizon, ensuring that technical scholarship remains accountable to the church's confession of the triune God who speaks through the prophetic and apostolic witness.

1.1 超越歷史主義碎片化：「聖經的神學詮釋」之興起

二十世紀末葉，聖經學界意識到一場認識論危機：歷史批判法的過度專業化，將語文學釋經與教會教義生命割裂。01 當聖經僅被視為由中立理性剖析的古代文獻時，釋經便淪為考古碎片，無力闡明經文如何作為三一上帝的活潑言說向當代發聲。「聖經的神學詮釋」（TIS）遂於九十年代興起，旨在恢復聖經作為「神聖經卷」的本體身分。02

在 TIS 本體論根基上，韋伯斯特（John Webster, 1955–2016）在《聖經：教義素描》（2003）與《聖言的領域》（2012）中力陳：釋經必須以「文本的神學本體論」為前提。03 聖經不能單靠世俗文本理論定義，因它存在於三一上帝自我溝通的恩典經世之中。上帝在主權的自有永有中，將受造語言分別為聖、保存並默示，使其成為基督臨在的媒介。教會本質上是creatura Verbi（「聖言的受造物」），其閱讀唯有作為對先在神聖言說的「專注聆聽」，方具正當性。04

在此視域下，TIS 核心學者提出互補方案：

  • 范胡澤（正典語言學與神聖戲劇）： 在《第一神學》（2002）、《教義的戲劇》（2005）與《巴別塔後的聖經權威》（2016）中，范胡澤建構三一溝通行動神學：聖父為發話者，聖子為神聖言內行動，聖靈則為使教會忠實演出聖經劇本的言後行動者。05
  • 特雷爾（催化性取回）： 在《神學詮釋學導論》（2008）與《神學與聖經之鏡》（2015）中，特雷爾指出 TIS 包含三重動能：(1) 取回教父象徵讀經；(2) 重視大公信經；(3) 倚靠聖靈在全球接收史中的光照。06
  • 福爾（讀者德性與多聲部字義）： 在《投入聖經》（1998）與《聖經的神學詮釋》（2009）中，福爾主張讀經目的乃深化與上帝及鄰舍之契合；援引阿奎那，論證字面意義具「受限多元性」，要求讀者具備謙卑與仁愛德性。07
  • 比林斯（改革宗聖約參與）： 在《上帝給上帝子民的話語》（2010）中，比林斯立足「與基督聯合」，將語法—歷史釋經嵌入救贖聖約戲劇。08

1.3 初代教父「信仰準則」（Regula Fidei）

貫穿 TIS 陣營的紐帶，是取回教父「信仰準則」（regula fidei）。愛任紐與特土良將其視為使徒對聖經以基督為中心之宏大敘事的內在精煉。09 依愛任紐「馬賽克鑲嵌畫」比喻，異端將君王寶石重組為狐狸或野狗，而 regula fidei 則是辨識君王真貌的原初藍圖，作為「詮釋的舵盤」（gubernaculum interpretationis），為原文釋經劃定三一與基督論邊界。

02 · SPEECH-ACT HERMENEUTICS 02 · 言語行動理論與溝通行動

Speech-Act Theory and Authorial Communicative Action: Austin, Searle, Vanhoozer, and Brown 言語行動理論與作者溝通行動：奧斯丁、塞爾、范胡澤與布朗

● HERMENEUTICAL AXIOM · ILLOCUTIONARY DETERMINACY 詮釋學公理 · 言內行動的確定性

Textual meaning is neither an inaccessible mental ghost inside the author's mind nor an arbitrary construct of reading communities; it is a public, rule-governed communicative act whose determinate sense resides in what the author did in saying what was written. 文本意義既非隱藏於作者腦海中的不可知心理幽靈，亦非讀者群體任意建構的產物；它是一種公開且受語言規約支配的溝通行動，其確定意義正錨定於作者「在說出該話語時所施行的行動」（言內行動）。

2.1 Ordinary Language Philosophy: J. L. Austin and John R. Searle

While TIS supplied the ecclesial and dogmatic horizon for contemporary exegesis, Speech-Act Theory provided the analytical philosophy of language required to defend determinate authorial meaning against postmodern skepticism.10 Originating in Oxford ordinary language philosophy through J. L. Austin's 1955 William James Lectures, published posthumously as How to Do Things with Words (1962), Speech-Act Theory dismantled the logical-positivist "descriptive fallacy"—the assumption that language functions solely to state empirically verifiable propositions.11 Austin demonstrated that speaking is fundamentally performative: to utter words under appropriate conventions is to perform social and interpersonal actions.

Austin articulated a tripartite anatomy of every communicative utterance:12 (1) the locutionary act, which is the phonetic, phatic, and rhetic act of saying something with determinate sense and reference; (2) the illocutionary act, which is the conventional communicative force enacted in saying something (e.g., promising, warning, commanding, absolving); and (3) the perlocutionary act, which comprises the consequential psychological or behavioral effects produced by saying something (e.g., persuading, comforting, alarming). Building on Austin, John R. Searle in Speech Acts (1969) and Expression and Meaning (1979) demonstrated that illocutionary acts are governed by constitutive rules and generate institutional facts, classifying all illocutions into five universal categories defined by their "directions of fit" between words and the world.13

2.2 Vanhoozer's Defense Against Deconstruction and the Intentional Fallacy

In Is There a Meaning in This Text? (1998), Kevin J. Vanhoozer mobilized Austin and Searle to counter Jacques Derrida's deconstructive différance (textual undecidability) and Stanley Fish's reader-response pragmatism ("texts don't mean; people mean with texts").14 Crucially, Vanhoozer—alongside Nicholas Wolterstorff—dismantled W. K. Wimsatt and Monroe Beardsley's famous "Intentional Fallacy" by exposing its underlying Cartesian psychologism: the flawed assumption that authorial intention is a private, inaccessible mental state behind the text.15

Drawing on G. E. M. Anscombe's Intention (1957), Vanhoozer argued that intention is a public, embodied, rule-governed communicative stance inscribed directly into the text's genre, syntax, and historical conventions. Against Fish, Vanhoozer formulated a decisive ethical axiom: "What an act counts as is not a matter of how it is taken, but of how it was meant."16 Conflating an author's illocution with a reader's perlocutionary reception commits hermeneutical violence and violates the Ninth Commandment against bearing false witness. Consequently, Vanhoozer redefined the classical Protestant sensus literalis as "the sum total of those illocutionary acts performed by the author intentionally and with self-awareness"—a definition that naturally encompasses metaphor, poetry, and irony because recognizing illocutionary force requires genre competence.17

2.3 Jeannine K. Brown, Perlocutionary Intention, and Canonical Double Agency

New Testament scholar Jeannine K. Brown operationalized speech-act categories for seminary exegesis in Scripture as Communication (2007). Brown introduced a vital distinction between actual, contingent perlocutionary effect (how empirical readers happen to respond) and the author's perlocutionary intention (the specific transformation, faith, or obedience the text is structurally designed to elicit).18 Epistles like Galatians or 1 Peter do not merely transmit locutionary data; they exhibit determinate teleological directedness. Furthermore, drawing on Nicholas Wolterstorff's Divine Discourse (1995), Vanhoozer, Brown, and Kit Barker articulate canonical double agency: God speaks through human writers via appropriated discourse (akin to a sovereign authorizing an ambassador's text), generating both historical human illocutions and a unified canonical illocutionary trajectory that climaxes in Christ. Within this framework, Vanhoozer notes that infallibility (God's Word accomplishes its communicative purpose) is logically prior to inerrancy (when that purpose is assertion, the proposition is true).

2.4 Hermeneutical Payoff: Resolving the Sensus Plenior Impasse

Speech-Act Theory also resolves one of evangelical hermeneutics' most persistent dilemmas: how to account for New Testament uses of the Old Testament and the classical concept of sensus plenior ("fuller sense") without resorting to arbitrary eisegesis or divorcing divine meaning from human authorship. Under Wolterstorff's and Vanhoozer's double-agency model, the human prophet's locutionary act and immediate historical illocution remain intact and determinate. Yet when God—as the supreme canonical Author—appropriates that human discourse within the unfolding redemptive canon, the divine Author can perform higher-order canonical illocutions (such as typological promise-fulfillment or christological witness) that are fully consonant with, though surpassing, the human writer's immediate historical horizon. Furthermore, by distinguishing illocutionary force from perlocutionary reception, the exegete can honor both the objective stability of the inspired text and the Spirit's dynamic role in illuminating readers across diverse cultural contexts. Exegesis must therefore trace both the local historical illocutionary act in its original covenantal setting and its canonical illocutionary resonance within the completed biblical witness.

2.1 日常語言哲學：奧斯丁與塞爾

若說 TIS 為當代釋經奠定了教會與教義視域，那麼「言語行動理論」（Speech-Act Theory）則提供了抵禦後現代懷疑論、捍衛確定作者意義的分析語言哲學基石。10 此理論源於牛津日常語言哲學派奧斯丁（J. L. Austin）《如何以言行事》（1962），擊破了邏輯實證主義假定語言僅用於陳述經驗命題的「描述謬誤」。11 語言在本質上是施為性的：在適當規約下說出話語，本身就是在施行社會行動。

奧斯丁將溝通話語剖析為三重行動：12 (1) 言表行動（locution），即「說出某話」的語音、句法與語義指涉；(2) 言內行動（illocution），即「在說出某話時所施行的行動」（如應許、警告、命令、赦免）；(3) 言後行動（perlocution），即「藉由說出某話在聽者身上產生的後果效應」（如說服、安慰）。塞爾（John R. Searle）於《言語行動》（1969）與《表達與意義》（1979）中進一步闡明，言內行動受「構成性規則」支配並創造「制度性事實」，且可依「話語與世界之契合方向」劃分為五大範疇。13

2.2 范胡澤對解構主義與「意圖謬誤」的反擊

在《這文本中有意義嗎？》（1998）中，范胡澤動員奧斯丁與塞爾反駁德希達的解構「延異」（différance）與費希的讀者反應論（「文本無意義，人以文本製造意義」）。14 更關鍵的是，范胡澤與沃特斯托夫拆解了「意圖謬誤」（Intentional Fallacy），指出該論點誤將作者意圖視為隱藏在文本背後、不可觸及的私人心理幽靈。15

援引安斯康姆（G. E. M. Anscombe）《意圖》（1957），范胡澤論證「意圖」乃是一種公開且受規約支配的溝通姿態，直接銘刻於文體與句法中。針對費希，范胡澤立下公理：「一個行動算作什麼，不取決於它如何被領受，而取決於它如何被意欲。」16 將言內行動與讀者反應混為一談，乃犯了第九誡的詮釋暴力。據此，范胡澤將「字面意義」（sensus literalis）界定為「作者有意識地在文本中施行的言內行動之總和」，此定義自然涵蓋隱喻、詩歌與反諷。17

2.3 布朗的「言後意圖」與正典雙重代理

新約學者布朗（Jeannine K. Brown）在《作為溝通的聖經》（2007）中區分了經驗讀者偶然產生的「言後效應」與作者在文本結構中刻意設計的「言後意圖」（perlocutionary intention）。18 此外，借鏡沃特斯托夫《神聖言說》（1995）的「徵用言說」，范胡澤、布朗與巴克建構了「正典雙重代理」理論：上帝如同君王授權使節文書般，徵用人類作者的言表與言內行動，在正典整體中織就指向基督的統一言內軌跡。在此框架下，無謬性（神聖言說必達成溝通目的）在邏輯上先於無誤性。

03 · PERICOPAL THEOLOGY 03 · 段落神學與文本整合

Pericopal Theology and Textual-Theological Integration: Kuruvilla, Kaiser, and the World in Front of the Text 段落神學與文本神學整合：庫魯維拉、凱澤與「文本前面的世界」

● INTEGRATIVE AXIOM · FROM PERICOPE TO CANON 整合公理 · 從經文段落邁向正典神學

Pericopal theology bridges atomistic grammatical-historical exegesis and synthetic dogmatics by discerning the specific theological thrust—the world in front of the text—enacted by each literary unit in its canonical setting. 「段落神學」（Pericopal Theology）在原子化的語法—歷史釋經與建構性的教義神學之間架起橋樑，透過辨識每一個獨立經文段落在其正典脈絡中所投射的特定神學施為力——即「文本前面的世界」——使釋經自然導向忠實的神學實踐。

3.1 The Missing Link Between Atomistic Exegesis and Systematic Theology

A persistent structural weakness in modern theological education has been the chasm between first-order philological exegesis and constructive theology. Exegetes frequently arrest their labor at lexical parsing, textual variants, and ancient Near Eastern parallels—producing antiquarian data without theological synthesis—while systematicians often extract isolated proof-texts (dicta probantia) stripped of their literary and redemptive-historical moorings.19 Contemporary integrated hermeneutics addresses this rupture by focusing on the pericope (περικοπή, "a delimited literary unit") as the primary theological-communicative unit of Scripture.20

3.2 Walter C. Kaiser Jr.: Single Authorial Meaning and the Principlizing Model

An influential evangelical precursor to pericopal integration is Walter C. Kaiser Jr. In Toward an Exegetical Theology (1981), Kaiser insisted on a strict hermeneutical discipline grounded in single authorial meaning (E. D. Hirsch's meaning vs. significance).21 To move legitimately from the ancient historical horizon to contemporary ecclesial ethics without collapsing into allegorism or subjectivism, Kaiser formulated the Principlizing Model and the Ladder of Abstraction:

  • Contextual Syntactical-Theological Analysis: Exegesis begins by isolating the author's overarching thesis and subordinate clauses within the pericope, refusing to import later canonical developments prematurely.
  • The Analogy of Antecedent Scripture: Against reading later New Testament systematics backward into Old Testament texts (analogy of faith), Kaiser mandated the analogy of antecedent Scripture—interpreting a passage strictly in light of the progressive revelation available up to that historical moment.22
  • The Ladder of Abstraction: The interpreter abstracts a timeless, transcultural theological principle directly from the specific historical command or narrative (moving upward from culturally bound detail to universal covenantal norm), and then re-applies that principle downward into the contemporary horizon.

3.3 Abraham Kuruvilla: Pericopal Theology, Ricoeur, and Christiconic Interpretation

While honoring Kaiser's commitment to textual authority, Abraham Kuruvilla in Privilege the Text! (2013) and A Vision for Preaching (2015) critiqued principlizing as overly cognitive and prone to reducing rich literary narratives into abstract, disembodied propositions.23 Drawing upon Paul Ricoeur's hermeneutical phenomenology (as mediated by Kevin J. Vanhoozer's Biblical Narrative in the Philosophy of Paul Ricoeur) and Speech-Act Theory, Kuruvilla proposed Pericopal Theology.24

Kuruvilla distinguishes between what the text says (its locutionary content, situated in the historical "world behind the text" and literary "world of the text") and what the text does (its illocutionary thrust, projecting a "world in front of the text"—an envisioned mode of covenantal existence into which the reader is summoned).25 Every pericope in the canon exhibits a specific theology of the pericope: not a generic systematic locus, nor a flat moral lesson, but a unique, Spirit-designed portrait of God's character and human faithfulness. Crucially, Kuruvilla crowns this model with Christiconic interpretation: every pericope, whether Old Testament law, wisdom, or Pauline paraenesis, displays a distinct facet of the image of Christ, to whose likeness the Holy Spirit conforms the church as it inhabits the world in front of the text.26

3.4 Tripartite Trajectory: Mediating Exegesis, Biblical Theology, and Systematics

In contemporary seminary curricula—exemplified by David J. Downs and Joel B. Green's Guidelines for Research and Exegetical Papers—pericopal theology serves as the indispensable mediating bridge across three disciplines.27 First, it honors grammatical-historical exegesis by refusing to bypass syntactic delimitation and discourse structure. Second, it feeds diachronic biblical theology by tracing how each pericope's unique theological thrust advances the covenantal storyline toward Christ. Third, it disciplines synchronic systematic theology by ensuring doctrinal formulations remain tethered to the actual illocutionary acts of canonical units.

3.5 Pedagogical and Homiletical Implications of Pericopal Integration

For seminary researchers and pastor-theologians alike, pericopal theology corrects two chronic pathologies in biblical exposition. On the one hand, it prevents lexical atomism—the temptation to construct sermons or papers out of disconnected word studies that ignore how the author's overarching argument unfolds across the entire literary unit. On the other hand, it guards against systematic flattening—the habit of treating every narrative or epistolary paragraph as a mere restatement of generic loci such as justification or sanctification. When integrated with Kaiser's insistence on respecting antecedent revelation and Vanhoozer's canonical theodrama, Kuruvilla's pericopal focus ensures that each literary unit retains its distinctive theological voice. By asking not merely "What does this sentence say?" but "What world in front of the text does this bounded pericope project, and how does it summon the covenant community to Christlikeness?", the interpreter preserves the irreducible literary particularity of each biblical unit while participating faithfully in the canonical drama of redemption.

3.2 凱澤（Walter C. Kaiser Jr.）：單一作者意義與「原則化模型」

在當代段落整合的先驅中，華特·凱澤於《邁向釋經神學》（Toward an Exegetical Theology, 1981）建立了嚴謹的方法論規範，堅守單一作者意義（承襲赫許「意義」與「關聯」之區分）。21 為使釋經者能正當地從古代歷史視域過渡到當代教會倫理，而不致墮入寓意解經或主觀主義，凱澤提出了「原則化模型」（Principlizing Model）與「抽象階梯」（Ladder of Abstraction）：

  • 上下文句法與神學分析： 釋經須先界定段落中作者的核心論點與從屬子句，拒絕過早強行讀入後期正典神學。
  • 先前聖經的類比（Analogy of Antecedent Scripture）： 反對以新約後期系統教義倒果為因地解讀舊約（即反對濫用「信仰類比」），凱澤要求嚴格遵守先前聖經的類比——僅依該歷史時刻以前已賜下的漸進啟示來詮釋經文。22
  • 抽象階梯： 從受特定文化與歷史條件限制的誡命或敘事出發，向上提煉出跨文化、永恆不變的神學原則，再向下應用於當代教會處境。

3.3 庫魯維拉（Abraham Kuruvilla）：段落神學、呂格爾與「基督聖像式」詮釋

儘管肯定凱澤對文本權威的委身，亞伯拉罕·庫魯維拉在《尊崇文本！》（Privilege the Text!, 2013）與《講道的異象》（2015）中指出，「原則化模型」過度偏向認知命題，容易將豐富生動的文學敘事抽乾為抽象、無血無肉的道德格言。23 借鏡保羅·呂格爾（Paul Ricoeur）的詮釋現象學（經由范胡澤《保羅·呂格爾哲學中的聖經敘事》所中介）以及言語行動理論，庫魯維拉建構了「段落神學」（Pericopal Theology）。24

庫魯維拉精細區分了文本「說了什麼」（即言表內容，座落於「文本背後的歷史世界」與「文本自身的文學世界」）以及文本「正在做什麼」（即言內施為力，投射出一個「文本前面的世界」〔world in front of the text〕——一個召喚讀者進入其中的聖約生命樣態）。25 正典中的每一個段落都展現出獨特的「段落神學」：它既非泛論式的系統教義，亦非扁平的道德說教，而是聖靈精心塑造的關於上帝性情與人類忠信的圖像。庫魯維拉進一步以「基督聖像式詮釋」（Christiconic interpretation）作為此模型的頂峰：無論是舊約律法、智慧文學抑或保羅書信的勸勉，每一段經文都折射出基督形象的一個獨特切面，聖靈正是藉此將棲居於「文本前面世界」的教會模塑成基督的樣式。26

Epistemic Controls I: Lexical, Semantic, and Grammatical Fallacy Auditing (Carson, Barr, Wettstein) 認識論防禦體系（一）：詞彙、語義與語法謬誤審核（卡森、巴爾與韋茨坦）

Words do not carry their entire theological dictionary in isolation; determinate lexical and grammatical meaning is governed synchronically by sentence syntax, discourse co-text, and first-century linguistic conventions. 詞彙在孤立狀態下並不承載整部神學辭典的總和；確定的詞彙與語法意義乃是由共時性的句子句法、語篇上下文（co-text）以及第一世紀的日常語言慣例所共同決定。

4.1 Lexical and Semantic Fallacies: Barr, Carson, and the Linguistic Turn

A theological interpretation of Scripture is only as epistemically credible as the linguistic exegesis on which it rests. Building on James Barr's landmark critique of Kittel's Theological Dictionary of the New Testament in The Semantics of Biblical Language (1961), D. A. Carson systematized a diagnostic taxonomy in Exegetical Fallacies (2nd ed., 1996) to inoculate interpreters against recurring semantic errors.28 As Barr and Carson demonstrated, words possess no inherent, mystical theological essence; they signify only within the syntactic and narrative structures of actual utterances.29

  • The Root Fallacy (Morphological Etymologizing): This fallacy assumes that a word's historical etymology or constituent morphemes govern its synchronic meaning. Classic examples include claiming that ἐκκλησία (ekklēsia, "assembly/church") means "called-out ones" solely from ἐκ + καλέω, or that ἀπολύτρωσις invariably foregrounds a commercial ransom payment even in contexts where it denotes simple deliverance.30 As David J. Downs and Joel B. Green mandate, interpreters must consult standard academic lexica (BDAG, HALOT) rather than concordances or etymological wordbooks.31
  • Illegitimate Totality Transfer (ITT): Coined by Barr and popularized by Carson, ITT occurs when an exegete imports the entire semantic range of a polysemous word across the canon into a single occurrence—for instance, loading all Pauline nuances of σάρξ (sarx, "flesh") or δίκαιος into a single verse where syntax activates only one specific sense.32
  • Semantic Anachronism and Historical Difference: Reading later English cognates or dogmatic systems back into ancient Koine terms—such as equating Paul's δύναμις (dynamis, "power") in Rom 1:16 with modern "dynamite," or retrojecting fifth-century notions of "paganism" into the first-century Mediterranean world—violates what eighteenth-century philologist Johann Jacob Wettstein termed "historical difference."33 Dale B. Martin usefully distinguishes "good anachronism" (self-consciously heuristic modern categories) from "bad anachronism" that distorts ancient social reality.34

4.2 Grammatical and Syntactical Fallacy Auditing

Equal vigilance is required at the level of morphology and syntax, where theological agendas frequently tempt interpreters to over-determine grammatical forms:35

  • Verbal Aspect Over-Determination: Treating the Greek aorist tense as invariably denoting "once-for-all" action (ignoring constative, ingressive, or gnomic uses) or forcing the Greek present tense always to signify "continuous, ongoing" action confuses Aktionsart (lexical/contextual action-type) with grammatical aspect.36
  • Genitive Over-Specification & False Disjunctions: Forcing a single rigid label onto ambiguous genitives (e.g., πίστις Χριστοῦ, subjective vs. objective genitive) without weighing discourse context, or constructing false "either-or" exclusions where biblical syntax permits "both-and" semantic overlap, distorts authorial illocutions.
  • Conditional & Articular Misreadings: Assuming first-class conditional clauses (εἰ + indicative) always mean "since" (reality) rather than "assuming for the sake of argument," or misapplying the Granville Sharp rule to plural or non-personal nouns, produces spurious doctrinal proofs.

4.3 Discourse-Level Controls: Guarding Against Atomistic Proof-Texting

Ultimately, lexical and grammatical fallacy controls converge on a single linguistic principle: meaning ascends from morpheme to clause, sentence, and whole discourse. Even an impeccable BDAG lexical entry or accurate morphological parse of a Greek participle fails if the interpreter detaches the clause from its macro-syntactic connectors (γάρ, οὖν, ἵνα, διό) and logical progression. D. A. Carson emphasizes that grammatical competence is a necessary but insufficient condition for valid exegesis; the interpreter must continually test micro-level lexical and syntactic hypotheses against the macro-level argument of the entire pericope and epistle. Similarly, comparative philology must respect Wettstein's historical difference, ensuring that Septuagintal (LXX) and documentary papyri evidence illuminate—rather than replace—the specific idiolect of the biblical writer. When lexical semantics, verbal aspect, and discourse connectors are audited together, the text's actual communicative constraints surface clearly, preventing theological presuppositions from overriding the author's inscribed syntax.

4.1 詞彙與語義謬誤：巴爾（James Barr）、卡森（D. A. Carson）與語言學轉向

  • 詞根謬誤（Root Fallacy / 詞態詞源學化）： 誤認一個詞的歷史詞源或構成詞素（morphemes）決定了它在共時文本中的意義。典型錯例包括僅憑 ἐκ ＋ καλέω 就斷言 ἐκκλησία（ekklēsia，「集會／教會」）必指「被召出來的一群人」，或無視上下文而硬將 ἀπολύτρωσις 一律解為商業「贖價交易」。30 誠如唐斯與格林所規範，學者分析希臘文詞義時，必須查考權威學術辭典（如 BDAG、HALOT），而非僅憑經文彙編或詞源手冊。31
  • 非法總體轉移（Illegitimate Totality Transfer, ITT）： 由巴爾命名並經卡森闡發，指釋經者將一個多義詞在全本正典中的所有潛在語義範疇，全部塞入單一經文出現處——例如將保羅在其他書信中對 σάρξ（「肉體」）或 δίκαιος（「義」）的全部神學發展，一次強加於句法僅啟動單一詞義的經節。32
  • 語義時代錯置（Semantic Anachronism）與「歷史差異」： 將後期英文同源詞或教義體系倒果為因地讀入古代通用希臘文——例如將羅馬書一章 16 節的 δύναμις（dynamis，「能力」）解為現代「炸藥」（dynamite），或將五世紀以後的「異教徒」（paganism）標籤強加於第一世紀地中海世界——均違背了十八世紀語文學家韋茨坦（Johann Jacob Wettstein）所強調的「歷史差異」（historical difference）。33 馬丁（Dale B. Martin）進一步區分了具啟發性的「良性時代錯置」與扭曲古代社會實在的「惡性時代錯置」。34

  • 所有格過度單一化與虛假二分（False Disjunctions）： 在缺乏語篇佐證下對模糊所有格（如 πίστις Χριστοῦ 的主格或受格之爭）強行貼上單一標籤，或在原文容許「兩者兼備」（both-and）時強行製造「非此即彼」（either-or）的排他對立，皆會扭曲作者的言內行動。

Valid historical comparison never jumps from superficial verbal resemblance to genealogical borrowing; it operates triadically (X resembles Y more than Z with respect to category C) and tests claims against saliency, distinctiveness, and negative absences. 有效的歷史比較絕不從表面的詞彙相似跳躍至譜系借用；它必須嚴格遵循三元比較公式（「X 在範疇 C 上比 Z 更像 Y」），並以顯著性、獨特性與反證性缺席（absences）進行檢驗。

5.1 Overcoming Parallelomania and Chronological Retrojection

Historical-grammatical exegesis frequently appeals to Second Temple Jewish, Greco-Roman, or ancient Near Eastern parallels to illuminate biblical backgrounds. Yet without strict comparative controls, background research degenerates into what Samuel Sandmel famously diagnosed in 1962 as parallelomania—the extravagant tendency to isolate superficial verbal resemblances between two texts and immediately assert direct literary dependence or genealogical borrowing.37

Drawing on historian of religions Jonathan Z. Smith (Drudgery Divine, 1990), John M. G. Barclay and B. G. White in The New Testament in Comparison (2020) established a rigorous comparative protocol.38 First, scholars must distinguish analogy (heuristic comparison of historically independent phenomena) from genealogy (demonstrable historical derivation). Second, valid comparison is never dyadic (merely juxtaposing X and Y) but always triadic: "X resembles Y more than Z with respect to category C," governed by disciplined selection, generalization, and critical redescription.39

Closely allied is the fallacy of chronological retrojection—treating late Rabbinic halakhah (Mishnah, Talmud) or second-century Gnostic writings as unmediated sources for first-century New Testament texts.40 To audit claims of direct literary dependence, Simon Gathercole proposes a three-part diagnostic matrix: (1) Saliency (the shared motif must be structurally weight-bearing in both texts, not a trivial aside); (2) Distinctiveness (the shared idiom must stand out against general contemporary literature); and (3) Absences ("the dog that didn't bark"—if core elements of an alleged source are wholly absent from the host text, genealogical claims collapse).41

5.2 Logical, Rhetorical, and Research-Design Safeguards (Carson, Gupta, Downs & Green)

Epistemic control extends beyond linguistics and history to the researcher's own cognitive posture and argumentative rhetoric. In Prepare, Succeed, Advance (2nd ed., 2019), Nijay K. Gupta highlights D. A. Carson's vital taxonomy of research design:42

  • Deductive Domestication vs. Inductive Data-Dumping: A deductive project tests a sharp hypothesis against the text, yielding focus—yet unless the exegete exercises relentless self-criticism, "the temptation to domesticate the evidence in order to defend the thesis becomes well-nigh irresistible." Conversely, an inductive approach gathers evidence evenhandedly without presuppositions, yet risks collapsing into "a lot of well-organized data" devoid of an arguable thesis.43
  • The Collage Fallacy & Proof by Citation: In Guidelines for Research and Exegetical Papers, David J. Downs and Joel B. Green warn against turning exegesis into a passive patchwork of secondary quotations (the "collage trap") or assuming that stacking footnotes proves a claim. As they incisively note, "Citing a secondary source proves nothing... An argument is judged on the basis of its treatment of the primary evidence, not on the list of persons who can be recruited to support it."44
  • Source Plundering & Epistemological Humility: Gupta likewise warns against "plundering" monographs for convenient isolated sentences without grasping their holistic argument, and against apologetic over-reaching that forces texts into premature dogmatic proof-texts. Finally, postgraduate exegetical standards mandate epistemological humility: eschewing hyperbolic modifiers ("unique," "central," "proves beyond doubt") in favor of calibrated probability claims.45

5.3 The Hermeneutical Virtue of Self-Falsification in Historical Research

Across both comparative backgrounds and argumentative rhetoric, the defining mark of mature Epoch 7 scholarship is the willingness to subject one's own exegetical hypothesis to rigorous self-falsification. Rather than hunting exclusively for confirming parallels or congenial secondary quotations, the disciplined researcher actively searches for counter-evidence, negative absences in comparative corpora, and alternative syntactic readings. As Jonathan Z. Smith and John M. G. Barclay remind New Testament interpreters, comparison is a scholarly act of redescription that illuminates difference no less than similarity. Moreover, Nijay K. Gupta's emphasis on reading complete monographs rather than plundering isolated pages fosters genuine familiarity with the scholarly community of interpretation. By combining Gathercole's negative criteria with Downs and Green's insistence on primary-text evidentiary priority, the exegete cultivates an epistemic integrity that honors both historical truth and theological accountability in every stage of research, producing arguments that withstand rigorous peer scrutiny across both biblical studies and systematic theology, while guarding the church against idiosyncratic readings.

5.1 克服「平行狂熱」（Parallelomania）與年代倒射

歷史—語法釋經經常援引第二聖殿時期猶太文獻、希羅哲學或古代近東文獻作為經文背景。然而，若缺乏嚴謹的比較方法論控制，背景研究極易墮落為桑德梅爾（Samuel Sandmel）於 1962 年所痛陳的「平行狂熱」（parallelomania）——即僅憑兩份文獻間表面的詞彙相似，便草率斷定其存在直接的文學依賴或譜系借用。37

借鏡宗教學家強納森·史密斯（Jonathan Z. Smith, Drudgery Divine, 1990）的理論，約翰·巴克萊（John M. G. Barclay）與懷特（B. G. White）在《比較中的新約》（The New Testament in Comparison, 2020）中確立了嚴格的比較規範。38 首先，學者必須區分「類比」（analogy，對歷史上彼此獨立之現象作啟發式比較）與「譜系」（genealogy，須證實歷史淵源與借用關係）。其次，有效的比較絕非二元的（僅並列 X 與 Y），而必然是三元的（triadic）：「在範疇 C 上，X 比 Z 更近似於 Y」，並受控於有意識的篩選、概化與批判性再描述。39

與此緊密相關的是「年代倒射」（chronological retrojection）謬誤——將晚近的拉比哈拉卡文獻（《米書拿》、《他勒目》）或二世紀諾斯底文獻，未經中介地直接當作一世紀新約文本的來源。40 為審核直接文學依賴的宣稱，蓋瑟科爾（Simon Gathercole）提出了三項診斷標準：(1) 顯著性（Saliency）：共同母題在雙方文本中皆須具結構性承重地位，而非微末枝節；(2) 獨特性（Distinctiveness）：共享詞彙或觀念須相對於當代一般文獻顯得獨特；(3) 缺席反證（Absences，「未吠之犬」）：若所謂「來源文本」的核心要素在宿主文本中完全缺席，則直接借用之說便不攻自破。41

  • 演繹式馴化證據 vs. 歸納式資料堆砌： 演繹式（deductive）研究從鮮明假設出發檢驗文本，雖具焦點，但除非學者具備極度嚴苛的自我批判精神，否則「為了捍衛論點而馴化（domesticate）證據的誘惑幾乎難以抗拒」；反之，歸納式（inductive）研究雖較客觀持平，卻容易淪為缺乏可辯論核心論點的「井然有序之資料堆砌」。43
  • 拼貼謬誤（Collage Fallacy）與「以引用證成」謬誤： 唐斯與格林在《研究與釋經論文指南》中嚴厲批判將論文寫成二手引文大雜燴的「拼貼陷阱」，以及誤以為堆砌註腳即可證明論點的迷思。他們指出：「引用二手文獻什麼也證明不了……一篇論證的價值取決於它對一手文本證據的處理，而非所能招募來背書的學者名單。」44

6.1 From Historical-Critical Fragmentation to Canonical-Communicative Synthesis

Epoch 7 represents not the abandonment of the grammatical-historical achievements of Epochs 4 through 6, but their critical sublation within a unified theological and linguistic architecture.46 When Theological Interpretation of Scripture (TIS), Speech-Act Theory, Pericopal Theology, and Epistemic Fallacy Controls are integrated into a single exegetical workflow, the interpreter avoids both the sterile positivism of secular historicism and the arbitrary subjectivism of postmodern reader-response.47 As Grant R. Osborne's "hermeneutical spiral" and Matthew R. 6.2 A Worked Paradigm: Philippians 2:5–11 Through the Six-Stage Integrated Workflow

To illustrate how these four contemporary pillars operate in concert, consider a postgraduate exegetical engagement with the Christ-hymn of Philippians 2:5–11:49

  • Stage 1 — Textual & Lexical-Syntactical Grounding (Fallacy-Audited): After verifying textual stability in NA28, the exegete audits key vocables such as μορφή (morphē), ἁρπαγμός (harpagmos), and κενόω (kenoō) using BDAG and first-century Koine usage, explicitly rejecting the Root Fallacy (e.g., resisting nineteenth-century kenoticist speculations that read later German metaphysics into ἑαυτὸν ἐκένωσεν) and Illegitimate Totality Transfer.50
  • Stage 2 — Discourse & Speech-Act Analysis: Moving from locution to illocutionary force, the interpreter recognizes that Paul does not quote Phil 2:6–11 merely as an abstract Christological lecture (Assertive). Governed by the opening Directive τοῦτο φρονεῖτε ἐν ὑμῖν ("have this mindset among yourselves," v. 5), the hymn functions as a covenantal exemplum whose perlocutionary intention (Jeannine Brown) is to dismantle factionalism and cultivate cruciform humility in the Philippian assembly.51
  • Stage 3 — Pericopal Theology (World in Front of the Text): Following Abraham Kuruvilla, the exegete articulates the pericope's specific theological thrust: the "world in front of the text" summons the church to inhabit the cruciform pattern of self-emptying service that mirrors the incarnate Lord (Christiconic interpretation), rather than extracting a generic moralism.52
  • Stage 4 — Diachronic Biblical Theology & Comparative Control: Applying Sandmel/Barclay/Gathercole controls, the interpreter traces Paul's intertextual echo of Isaiah 45:23 (πᾶν γόνυ κάμψῃ, "every knee shall bow") without committing parallelomania toward Greco-Roman ruler cults, demonstrating how Paul identifies Jesus with YHWH's eschatological sovereignty.53
  • Stage 5 — Synchronic Systematic Theology & Regula Fidei: Guided by Webster, Treier, and the patristic regula fidei, the exegete situates Paul's high Christology within the Trinitarian confession of Chalcedon and Nicaea—guarding against subordinationist misreadings while keeping dogmatic synthesis anchored to Paul's actual illocutions.54

6.1 從歷史批判的碎片化邁向正典溝通的綜合

第七時期並非對第四至第六時期語法—歷史釋經成就的拋棄，而是將其批判性地揚棄（sublation）並整合於統一的神學與語言學架構之中。46 當「聖經的神學詮釋」（TIS）、言語行動理論、段落神學與認識論謬誤防禦體系匯流為單一的釋經實作流時，詮釋者便能同時避開世俗歷史主義的貧瘠實證論，以及後現代讀者反應論的任意主觀主義。47 誠如奧斯本（Grant R. Osborne）的「詮釋學螺旋」與馬爾科姆（Matthew R. Malcolm）從詮釋學邁向釋經的軌跡所示，嚴謹方法論與神學領受性並非敵對，而是相輔相成。48

6.2 實作典範：以六階整合工作流解析《腓立比書》二章 5–11 節

為具體展示上述四大當代支柱如何協同運作，茲以神學研究院層級對《腓立比書》二章 5–11 節（基督頌）之釋經為例：49

  • 第一階——文本與詞彙句法奠基（經謬誤審核）： 在 NA28 確認文本穩定性後，釋經者依據 BDAG 與第一世紀通用希臘文語料審核 μορφή（形像）、ἁρπαγμός（強奪／抓取）與 κενόω（倒空）等關鍵詞，明確拒絕詞根謬誤（例如抵制十九世紀「虛己論」將後期德國形上學強行讀入 ἑαυτὸν ἐκένωσεν）與非法總體轉移。50
  • 第二階——語篇與言語行動分析： 從言表行動推進至言內施為力，詮釋者辨識保羅引述此詩歌並非僅為進行抽象的基督論講授（斷言類）。受開端指令句 τοῦτο φρονεῖτε ἐν ὑμῖν（「你們當以基督耶穌的心為心」，第 5 節）支配，此頌歌作為聖約典範（exemplum）運作，其「言後意圖」（布朗）乃在於瓦解腓立比教會的結黨紛爭並塑造十架謙卑。51
  • 第三階——段落神學（文本前面的世界）： 依循庫魯維拉的模型，釋經者闡明該段落獨特的神學推進力：「文本前面的世界」召喚教會棲居於效法道成肉身之主自我倒空服事的十架樣式（基督聖像式詮釋），而非抽離出扁平的道德說教。52
  • 第四階——歷時聖經神學與比較控制： 運用桑德梅爾／巴克萊／蓋瑟科爾的比較防線，釋經者追蹤保羅對《以賽亞書》四十五章 23 節（πᾶν γόνυ κάμψῃ，「萬膝必跪拜」）的正典文本互涉，同時避免對希羅帝王崇拜犯下平行狂熱，論證保羅如何將耶穌納入雅威的末世主權之中。53
  • 第五階——共時系統神學與「信仰準則」： 在韋伯斯特、特雷爾與初代教父 regula fidei 的引導下，釋經者將保羅的高位基督論置於尼西亞與迦克墩的三一認信框架中——既防範次位論的誤讀，又確保教義綜合始終緊扣保羅文本的真實言內行動。54
