01 · HISTORIOGRAPHY & DISCIPLINARY MEMORY 01 · 詮釋史學與學科記憶

The Historiography of Biblical Interpretation: Beyond Antiquarian Chronicle 聖經詮釋史學：超越古物誌編年史的認識論任務

● METHODOLOGICAL AXIOM · WIRKUNGSGESCHICHTE 方法論公理 · 效果歷史意識

The history of biblical interpretation is not an optional museum of exegetical curiosities; because every reader inherits a linguistically and confessionally mediated tradition, critical historiography is an indispensable internal control on contemporary exegesis. 聖經詮釋史絕非可有可無的釋經奇談博物館；由於每位讀者皆繼承了一套受語言與認信傳統中介的視域，批判性的詮釋史學乃是當代釋經不可或缺的內在控制機制。

1.1 Against Chronological Snobbery in Biblical Studies

A persistent temptation in modern biblical scholarship is the assumption that rigorous exegesis began only with the rise of twentieth-century critical methods or recent linguistic breakthroughs. As Nijay K. Gupta observes in his doctoral manual, younger researchers frequently operate as though Pauline or Gospel studies commenced with late-twentieth-century paradigms, remaining largely unaware of foundational secondary literature written prior to 1980.01 This form of chronological amnesia impoverishes exegesis in two ways: it blinds interpreters to the genealogy of their own methodological presuppositions, and it deprives them of hard-won philological and theological insights forged across two millennia of Jewish and Christian engagement with Scripture.02

In contrast to antiquarian chronicle, the historiography of biblical interpretation investigates how interpretive communities across distinct eras—Second Temple synagogues, patristic bishoprics, medieval cathedral schools, Reformation academies, and modern research universities—asked divergent questions of the same canonical corpus, deployed contrasting methodological protocols, and thereby disclosed or obscured specific dimensions of the text.03 As David J. Downs emphasizes in his graduate research guidelines, every exegete writes within an ongoing "community of interpretation" and must distinguish primary textual evidence from secondary interpretive reception while remaining accountable to both.04

1.2 Major Historiographical Genealogies and Taxonomic Architecture

Contemporary historiography of exegesis is anchored by multi-volume critical syntheses that map the diachronic evolution of interpretive method. William Baird's two-volume History of New Testament Research traces the emergence of historical-critical consciousness from Deism and Johann Salomo Semler through the Tübingen school and Rudolf Bultmann, demonstrating how philosophical shifts repeatedly reconfigured New Testament theology.05 Parallel to Baird, Alan J. Hauser, Duane F. Watson, and Schuyler Kaufman's multi-volume A History of Biblical Interpretation charts the sweep from inner-biblical exegesis, the Septuagint, Qumran, and Philo through the medieval, Reformation, and Enlightenment periods, while Rudolf Smend's From Astruc to Zimmerli documents the European trajectory of Old Testament criticism.06

Complementing these diachronic histories, Stephen Neill and N. T. Wright's The Interpretation of the New Testament 1861–1986 and Donald K. McKim's Dictionary of Major Biblical Interpreters demonstrate that interpretive methods never emerge in an intellectual vacuum.07 Rather, exegetical revolutions are catalysed by broader crises in epistemology, philology, political authority, and ecclesial self-understanding. A mature hermeneutical historiography therefore refuses to treat pre-critical interpreters as naive literalists or allegorists; instead, it reconstructs the internal coherence of each epoch's regula (governing rule), assesses how its philological and philosophical tools interacted with its ecclesial or synagogue context, and evaluates its explanatory power against the original-language text.

1.3 Reception History, the Implied Reader, and Primary vs. Secondary Horizons

In recent decades, Markus Bockmuehl has argued in Seeing the Word that historical-critical scholarship trapped itself in an antiquarian cul-de-sac by treating the New Testament as an inert archaeological artifact detached from its living reception.08 Bockmuehl contends that the New Testament documents were written for an implied reader situated within the apostolic community—a reader who shares the presupposition of Christ's resurrection and the Spirit's presence—and that authentic exegesis must therefore attend to the text's Wirkungsgeschichte (effective history), especially the earliest patristic and ecclesial reception.09

At the same time, historiographical rigor demands a clear boundary between what an ancient text communicated in its original communicative situation and how subsequent communities re-appropriated that text. By holding primary philological exegesis and secondary reception history in disciplined tension, the historian of interpretation exposes hidden dogmatic projections, recovers forgotten textual possibilities, and equips the contemporary interpreter to enter the biblical text with methodological self-awareness. Without this methodological distinction, reception history collapses into relativistic reader-response, treating every later doctrinal or homiletical gloss as equally valid to the author's original linguistic act; conversely, without reception history, historical-critical exegesis degenerates into sterile antiquarianism that ignores how canonical texts have actually shaped ecclesial liturgy, ethics, and theology across centuries. In practical terms, this means reading primary sources in their original Hebrew, Aramaic, Greek, and Latin idioms alongside the classic commentaries of Chrysostom, Augustine, Aquinas, Luther, and Calvin, testing every inherited reading against lexical semantics, syntactic structure, and ancient Mediterranean socio-historical realia. Only through such disciplined two-way accountability can contemporary exegesis avoid both uncritical traditionalism and rootless novelty. Indeed, as Stanley E. Porter's taxonomy of fourteen exegetical methods demonstrates, contemporary biblical scholarship is itself a historically layered enterprise in which textual criticism, synchronic linguistics, rhetorical analysis, social-scientific modeling, and reception history operate as complementary instruments rather than mutually exclusive ideologies.

1.1 拒絕聖經研究中的「時代傲慢」

當代聖經學術界常見的一種誘惑，是假定嚴謹的釋經直到二十世紀歷史批判法成熟或晚近語言學轉向之後才真正開始。誠如古普塔（Nijay K. Gupta）在其博士研究指南中所指出，年輕研究者往往彷彿認為保羅書信或福音書研究始於二十世紀末的學術典範，對 1980 年以前的關鍵二手文獻卻鮮少涉獵。01 這種「時代失憶症」從兩方面削弱了釋經的深度：一方面使詮釋者盲目於自身方法論預設的歷史系譜，另一方面則使其錯失兩千年來猶太會堂與基督教會在文本角力中所累積的語文學與神學洞見。02

與單純羅列古籍的「古物誌編年史」不同，聖經詮釋史學（historiography of biblical interpretation）旨在考察不同歷史時期的詮釋群體——從第二聖殿會堂、教父主教座堂、中世紀經院學府、宗教改革學院到現代研究型大學——如何向同一部正典提出截然不同的問題，部署相異的方法論規範，並藉此揭示或遮蔽了文本的特定維度。03 正如唐斯（David J. Downs）在研究院論文規範中所強調，每位釋經者皆置身於一個延續不斷的「詮釋群體」（community of interpretation）之中，必須嚴格區分一手文本證據與二手詮釋領受，並同時對兩者負責。04

1.2 主要詮釋史學系譜與分類架構

當代聖經詮釋史學立基於多卷本的批判性綜合研究之上，系統性地繪製出釋經方法歷時演變的地圖。貝爾德（William Baird）兩卷本的《新約研究史》（History of New Testament Research）追溯了歷史批判意識如何從自然神論與塞姆勒（Johann Salomo Semler）興起，經由杜賓根學派推進至布特曼，證明哲學典範的轉移如何反覆重塑新約神學。05 與之並行的還有豪瑟（Alan J. Hauser）、華生（Duane F. Watson）與考夫曼（Schuyler Kaufman）合編的多卷本《聖經詮釋史》（A History of Biblical Interpretation），橫跨正典內部釋經、七十士譯本、昆蘭古卷、斐羅、中世紀、宗教改革與啟蒙運動；而斯門德（Rudolf Smend）的《從阿斯特呂克到齊默利》（From Astruc to Zimmerli）則勾勒出歐洲舊約批判學的發展軌跡。06

此外，尼爾（Stephen Neill）與賴特（N. T. Wright）的《新約詮釋史（1861–1986）》以及麥金（Donald K. McKim）主編的《主要聖經詮釋者辭典》，皆進一步證實釋經方法從不在思想真空中誕生。07 相反地，釋經典範的革命往往由認識論、語文學、政治權威與教會自我理解的深層危機所觸發。因此，成熟的詮釋史學拒絕將前批判時期的解經家簡化為天真的字面主義者或隨意的寓意解經者；它致力於重建每個時期內在規則（regula）的邏輯連貫性，並評估其解釋力。

1.3 領受史、隱含讀者與一手／二手視域之界線

近年來，博克穆爾（Markus Bockmuehl）在《看見聖言》（Seeing the Word）中指出，現代歷史批判學因將新約視為與活潑信仰群體脫節的考古遺物，已使自身陷入「古物主義的死胡同」。08 博克穆爾論證，新約文獻本是為置身使徒群體內的「隱含讀者」（implied reader）而寫——該讀者共享基督復活與聖靈同在的核心預設——因此真實的釋經必須正視文本的Wirkungsgeschichte（效果歷史），特別是早期教父與教會群體的見證。09

與此同時，史學的嚴謹性要求我們在「古代文本於原初溝通處境中所傳達的意義」與「後世群體如何重新挪用該文本」之間劃出清晰界線。唯有將第一序的原文語法釋經與第二序的領受史保持在受紀律約束的張力中，詮釋史學家方能揭露隱藏的教義投射，重拾被遺忘的文本潛能，並裝備當代釋經者以高度的方法論自覺進入聖經文本。若缺乏此一方法論區分，領受史便會淪為相對主義的讀者反應論，將後世所有教義或講道衍生皆視為與作者原初語言行動同等有效；反之，若完全忽略領受史，歷史批判釋經則會退化為貧瘠的古物主義，無視正典文本歷代以來對教會禮儀、倫理與神學的真實塑造。在具體治學實踐上，這意味著研究者既須以希伯來文、亞蘭文、希臘文與拉丁文一手文獻為本，亦須與屈梭多模、奧古斯丁、阿奎那、路德與加爾文等經典註釋展開對話，並以詞彙語義學、句法結構及古代地中海社會歷史實況檢驗每一項承繼而來的解讀。

02 · PARADIGMS & HORIZON FUSION 02 · 典範轉移與視域融合

Kuhn's Paradigm Shifts and Gadamer's Philosophical Hermeneutics 孔恩的「典範轉移」與高達美的「哲學詮釋學」

● CORE SYNTHESIS · HORIZONTVERSCHMELZUNG 核心綜合 · 視域融合之認識論結構

Hermeneutical history advances neither by pure cumulative induction nor by arbitrary relativism, but through Kuhnian paradigm crises and Gadamerian horizon-fusions in which the interpreter's pre-understanding is tested by the textual otherness of Scripture. 詮釋史既非純粹線性累積的歸納過程，亦非任意的相對主義；它乃透過孔恩式的典範危機與高達美式的「視域融合」向前推進，使詮釋者的前理解不斷受聖經文本之「他者性」的檢驗與重塑。

2.1 Thomas S. Kuhn: Normal Exegesis, Anomalies, and Paradigm Shifts

To explain why biblical interpretation undergoes tectonic realignments across centuries rather than smooth, linear refinement, hermeneutical theorists—notably Anthony C. Thiselton, Grant R. Osborne, and William W. Klein, Craig L. Blomberg, and Robert L. Hubbard Jr.—have adapted Thomas S. Kuhn's The Structure of Scientific Revolutions.10 Kuhn argued that mature intellectual disciplines operate within a shared paradigm (disciplinary matrix): a constellation of foundational beliefs, canonical exemplars, and methodological rules that define what counts as a legitimate question and an acceptable solution.11 During periods of "normal science," practitioners solve puzzles within the inherited matrix without questioning its bedrock axioms.

However, as researchers press their methods into new philological or historical data, persistent anomalies emerge that the reigning paradigm cannot resolve without ad hoc contortions. When anomalies accumulate—such as Renaissance humanists recovering Hebrew and Greek manuscripts that exposed Latin Vulgate mistranslations and late-medieval allegorical excess, or twentieth-century scholars confronting the theological sterility of positivist source-criticism—the discipline enters a state of crisis.12 A paradigm shift occurs when a rival community proposes a reconfigured explanatory matrix (for example, the Reformation's sola Scriptura and sensus literalis, or the Enlightenment's historicist triad) that resolves the crisis while redefining the standards of rationality itself.13

2.2 Hans-Georg Gadamer: Rehabilitation of Prejudice and Effective History

Whereas Kuhn illuminates the sociological and institutional dynamics of interpretive revolutions, Hans-Georg Gadamer's Wahrheit und Methode (Truth and Method, 1960) uncovers the ontological conditions of human understanding.14 Building upon Martin Heidegger's existential phenomenology in Sein und Zeit (1927)—specifically the triadic fore-structure of understanding comprising Vorhabe (fore-having), Vorsicht (fore-sight), and Vorgriff (fore-conception)—Gadamer dismantled the Enlightenment's "prejudice against prejudice" (Vorurteil).15 No interpreter approaches an ancient text as a blank slate (tabula rasa); rather, Vorverständnis (pre-understanding) and inherited tradition are the indispensable, productive conditions that make any initial comprehension possible.

Gadamer further demonstrated that every reader stands inside Wirkungsgeschichte (effective history): the continuous stream of historical effects, translations, commentaries, and doctrinal formulations generated by the text across time.16 As Gadamer famously formulated, "History does not belong to us; we belong to it." Recognizing one's temporal finitude and hermeneutical opacity gives rise to wirkungsgeschichtliches Bewusstsein (historically effected consciousness), which humbly acknowledges that absolute, ahistorical transparency is unattainable for mortal interpreters.

2.3 Fusion of Horizons (Horizontverschmelzung) and Habermas's Ideology Critique

Gadamer's central metaphor for cross-temporal understanding is the fusion of horizons (Horizontverschmelzung).17 A "horizon" is the range of vision that includes everything visible from a particular historical vantage point. Understanding an ancient biblical text requires neither the impossible psychological self-annihilation of Romantic empathy nor the imperialistic assimilation of the text into modern categories. Instead, it occurs as a dialogical encounter of question and answer in which the horizon of the ancient text—its linguistic, covenantal, and historical otherness (Andersheit)—confronts and interrogates the interpreter's contemporary horizon, producing an expanded, newly integrated horizon.

In the celebrated Gadamer–Habermas debate of the 1960s and 1970s, Jürgen Habermas warned that Gadamer's deference to tradition risks legitimizing social pathology and systematically distorted communication.18 Habermas championed Ideologiekritik and the "transcending power of reflection" (die transzendierende Kraft der Reflexion)—the critical capacity of reason to step back from inherited authority and expose hidden power interests. When synthesized with Kuhnian paradigm analysis, Gadamerian horizon-fusion and Habermasian critical reflection provide the historiographer with a dual lens: honoring tradition as the matrix of understanding while subjecting every interpretive paradigm to rigorous philological and ethical critique. Moreover, Gadamer's emphasis on the dialogical structure of question and answer clarifies why identical biblical texts generate fresh theological insights when interrogated by new historical crises: every era poses distinct questions from its own horizon, yet the text's linguistic determinacy sets principled boundaries on what counts as a faithful answer.

2.1 孔恩：常態釋經、異象累積與典範轉移

為解釋聖經詮釋史何以在數個世紀間經歷板塊式的結構重組，而非平順線性的微調，當代詮釋學家——尤以西瑟頓（Anthony C. Thiselton）、奧斯本（Grant R. Osborne），以及克萊恩、布隆伯格與哈伯德（Klein, Blomberg, and Hubbard）為代表——相繼引入孔恩（Thomas S. Kuhn）《科學革命的結構》之理論架構。10 孔恩指出，成熟的學術社群通常運作於一個共享的「典範」（paradigm）或學科矩陣之中：即一套決定何謂正當問題與有效解答的信念、經典範例與方法論規範。11 在「常態科學」時期，學者只在既定框架內解謎，而不會質疑其根基公理。

然而，當研究者將既有方法應用於新發現的語文學或歷史證據時，便會浮現現行典範無法消解的頑強「異象」（anomalies）。當異象累積到臨界點——例如文藝復興人文主義者重拾希伯來文與希臘文抄本，揭露拉丁武加大譯本的誤譯與中世紀末期寓意解經的濫用；或二十世紀學者面對實證主義來源批判在神學上的貧瘠——學科便陷入「危機」（crisis）。12 此時，若有競爭性的詮釋社群提出重組後的解釋矩陣（如宗教改革的 sola Scriptura 與 sensus literalis，或啟蒙運動的歷史主義三原則），便會引發「典範轉移」（paradigm shift），同時重新定義理性與有效的標準。13

2.2 高達美：成見的平反與效果歷史意識

如果說孔恩照明了詮釋革命的社會學與制度動力，高達美（Hans-Georg Gadamer）的《真理與方法》（Wahrheit und Methode, 1960）則揭示了人類理解的本體論條件。14 承繼海德格（Martin Heidegger）在《存在與時間》（Sein und Zeit, 1927）中對理解前結構（Vorhabe 前擁有、Vorsicht 前視角、Vorgriff 前概念）的分析，高達美徹底拆解了啟蒙運動「反對成見的成見」（Vorurteil）。15 沒有任何詮釋者能以「白板」（tabula rasa）之姿進入古代文本；相反地，前理解（Vorverständnis）與所繼承的傳統，正是使初步理解得以發生的不可或缺且具生產力的條件。

高達美進一步闡明，每位讀者皆置身於「效果歷史」（Wirkungsgeschichte）之內——即文本歷經數世紀所產生的譯本、註釋書、禮儀與教義之持續效應流。16 正如高達美的名言：「歷史並不屬於我們；我們屬於歷史。」體認自身的時間有限性與詮釋不透明性，便會孕育出「效果歷史意識」（wirkungsgeschichtliches Bewusstsein），謙卑地承認有限的凡人無法達致絕對、超歷史的透明理解。

2.3 「視域融合」與哈伯馬斯的意識形態批判

高達美用以描述跨時代理解的核心隱喻是「視域融合」（Horizontverschmelzung）。17 所謂「視域」，是指從特定歷史立足點所能見及的一切視野範圍。理解古代聖經文本，既不要求浪漫主義心理學那種不可能的自我消弭，也不容許將文本粗暴同化於現代範疇；相反地，它是在問答辯證的相遇中發生的事件：古代文本以其語言、聖約與歷史的「他者性」（Andersheit）質問並修正詮釋者的當代視域，進而生成一個更為開闊、整合的新視域。

在 1960 至 1970 年代著名的「高達美—哈伯馬斯論戰」中，哈伯馬斯（Jürgen Habermas）提醒，過度順從傳統恐將合理化社會病態與系統性扭曲的溝通。18 哈伯馬斯倡導「意識形態批判」（Ideologiekritik）與「反思的超越力量」（die transzendierende Kraft der Reflexion），主張理性具備抽離既定權威並揭露隱藏權力利益的批判能力。當孔恩的典範分析與高達美的視域融合、哈伯馬斯的批判反思相結合時，詮釋史學便獲得了一副雙重鏡片：既尊重傳統作為理解的母體，又將每一詮釋典範置於嚴格的語文學與倫理批判之下。此外，高達美對問答辯證結構的強調，亦闡明了何以同一部聖經文本在面對新的歷史危機時，能不斷湧現鮮活的神學洞見：每個時代皆從自身視域提出獨特問題，而文本的語言確定性則為忠實的回答劃定原則性邊界。

03 · SEVEN EPOCHS OVERVIEW 03 · 七大時期宏觀架構

Macro-Architecture of the Seven Hermeneutical Epochs 聖經詮釋七大歷史時期之宏觀典範架構

● HISTORICAL TRAJECTORY · SEVEN HORIZONS 歷史軌跡 · 七大詮釋視域之演進

Across seven distinct epochs—from Second Temple covenantal actualization to contemporary critical realism—every hermeneutical paradigm is defined by how it negotiates the triangle of historical distance, literary sense, and theological authority. 橫跨七大歷史時期——從第二聖殿的聖約當代化到當代的批判實在論——每一個詮釋典範的本質，皆取決於它如何協調「歷史距離」、「文學字義」與「神學權威」所構成的三角張力。

3.1 Diachronic Periodization: From Inner-Biblical Exegesis to Integrated Synthesis

To map the two-millennium trajectory of biblical interpretation without flattening its internal diversity, Part I of this compendium organizes the history of hermeneutics into seven heuristic epochs.19 Each epoch represents a recognizable Kuhnian disciplinary matrix characterized by distinctive philological tools, philosophical assumptions, and communal loci of authority. Yet these periods are neither hermetically sealed nor strictly sequential; earlier strategies frequently persist as recessive or contested strands within subsequent eras.20

Epoch 1 (Inner-Biblical & Second Temple Judaism) demonstrates that interpretation begins inside the Hebrew canon itself through the dialectic of traditum and traditio, before branching into rabbinic midrash, Qumranic charismatic pesher, and Philonic philosophical allegory.21 Epoch 2 (Patristic Hermeneutics) marks the decisive Christological reconfiguration of the Old Testament (Luke 24:27), generating the enduring polarity between Alexandrian tripartite allegoria and Antiochene theoria under the doctrinal boundary of the regula fidei.22 Epoch 3 (Medieval Scholasticism) codifies the fourfold Quadriga within the Glossa Ordinaria, even as Thomas Aquinas and Nicholas of Lyra reassert the foundational primacy of the sensus literalis.23

3.2 Modern Ruptures and Contemporary Reintegrations (Epochs 4–7)

Epoch 4 (The Protestant Reformation) executes a sharp paradigm shift driven by Renaissance ad fontes philology and theological crisis, repudiating arbitrary allegorization as a "wax nose" (nasus cereus) in favor of sola Scriptura, claritas Scripturae, and the single, natural literal sense.24 Epoch 5 (The Enlightenment & Historical-Critical Paradigm) introduces a profound epistemological rupture: Baruch Spinoza, Johann Salomo Semler, and Ernst Troeltsch sever historical meaning (sensus) from theological truth (veritas), confining exegesis within an immanent, naturalistic continuum that alienates the ancient text from the contemporary church.25

Epoch 6 (Twentieth-Century Philosophical, Linguistic & Canonical Turns) dismantles Enlightenment positivism through Karl Barth's dialectical theology, Martin Heidegger and Hans-Georg Gadamer's ontological hermeneutics, Paul Ricoeur's narrative arc, and Brevard S. Childs's canonical approach.26 Finally, Epoch 7 (Contemporary Integrated Exegesis) seeks a disciplined bilateral synthesis—uniting historical-grammatical rigor, synchronic discourse linguistics, speech-act theory, and canonical theology under the epistemological posture of critical realism.27 Across all seven epochs, two structural patterns emerge for the historiographer. First, no paradigm shift ever completely erases its predecessors: Second Temple typological correlations resurface in Patristic theoria, Thomistic sensus literalis anticipates Reformation philology, and Enlightenment linguistic precision remains indispensable to contemporary critical realism. Second, every major crisis in hermeneutical history has arisen when an interpretive community either absolutized its own temporal horizon (producing anachronistic eisegesis) or severed historical inquiry from the theological subject matter (die Sache) of the canon. The sevenfold taxonomy below therefore serves not merely as a chronological map, but as a diagnostic grid for evaluating how each era navigated text, history, and community. By comparing how each epoch construes historia, Vorverständnis, and horizon encounter, the doctoral researcher gains an analytical vocabulary for diagnosing contemporary methodological debates and situating any exegetical proposal within its deeper historical trajectory. Specifically, this comparative lens prevents the common error of mistaking a local, historically contingent interpretive habit—such as Enlightenment anti-supernaturalism or late-medieval allegorism—for a timeless, self-evident axiom of textual meaning, reminding interpreters that every reading strategy carries implicit ontological commitments.

3.1 歷時分期：從正典內部釋經到整合性綜合

為在不抹平內部多樣性的前提下測繪兩千年聖經詮釋史的軌跡，本典籍第一部將詮釋史劃分為七個啟發式時期（seven heuristic epochs）。19 每個時期皆代表一個可辨識的孔恩式學科矩陣，具備獨特的語文學工具、哲學預設與群體權威重心。然而，這些時期既非彼此封閉，亦非單純線性替代；較早期的策略往往在後續時代中以潛流或受爭辯的傳統持續運作。20

第一時期（正典內部與第二聖殿猶太教）揭示了詮釋行動始於希伯來正典內部traditum（受領傳統）與traditio（傳承詮釋）的辯證，繼而分化為拉比「米德拉什」（midrash）、昆蘭靈恩「佩舍」（pesher）與斐羅哲學寓意解經。21 第二時期（教父時期）標誌著以基督事件重構舊約的決定性轉向（路 24:27），在regula fidei（信仰準則）的教義邊界內，催生出亞歷山大派三重allegoria與安提阿派theoria的長期張力。22 第三時期（中世紀經院哲學）將「四重意義法」（Quadriga）系統化於《標準註釋》（Glossa Ordinaria）中，同時阿奎那與里拉的尼古拉重新確立了sensus literalis（字面意義）的根基地位。23

3.2 現代斷裂與當代再整合（第四至七時期）

第四時期（宗教改革）在文藝復興ad fontes（回到源頭）語文學與教會危機的雙重驅動下，發動了劇烈的典範轉移：斥責任意寓意化為「蠟製鼻子」（nasus cereus），轉而高舉sola Scriptura、claritas Scripturae與單一自然的字面原意。24 第五時期（啟蒙運動與歷史批判典範）則帶來深刻的認識論斷裂：斯賓諾莎、塞姆勒與特勒爾奇將歷史意義（sensus）與神學真理（veritas）徹底切割，使釋經受困於封閉的自然主義因果網，造成古代文本與當代教會的疏離。25

第六時期（二十世紀哲學、語言學與正典轉向）透過巴特的辯證神學、海德格與高達美的本體論詮釋學、呂格爾的敘事弧以及柴爾茲（Brevard S. Childs）的正典進路，瓦解了啟蒙實證主義的霸權。26 最終，第七時期（當代整合釋經）致力於建立雙向綜合——在「批判實在論」的認識論立場下，將歷史文法嚴謹性、共時語篇語言學、言語行動理論與正典神學融為一體。27 縱觀這七大時期，詮釋史學家可辨識出兩項深層結構規律：首先，任何典範轉移從未徹底抹除其前驅——第二聖殿的預表關聯在教父「靈視」（theoria）中重現，托馬斯的字面優先論為宗教改革語文學鋪路，而啟蒙時期所鍛造的語言精確性至今仍是當代批判實在論不可或缺的基石；其次，詮釋史上每一次重大危機，皆源於詮釋群體要麼將自身的時代視域絕對化（導致時代錯置的私意強解），要麼將歷史考察與正典的神學實質主題（die Sache）強行割裂。因此，下述七重分類矩陣不僅是歷時地圖，更是診斷各時代如何協調文本、歷史與群體的精密羅盤。

Continuity and Discontinuity in Pre-Modern Reading: Literal, Typological, and Allegorical Dialectics 前現代閱讀中的延續與斷裂：字義、預表與寓意的歷史辯證

Authentic typology (typos) preserves historical reality (historia) through covenantal correspondence and redemptive escalation, whereas Hellenistic allegorism treats historical particulars as disposable ciphers for timeless philosophical or dogmatic abstractions. 真正的「預表論」（typos）透過聖約對應與救贖升級來保全歷史實在性（historia）；相對地，希臘化寓意解經往往將具體歷史事件視為可拋棄的符號，以換取超時間的哲學或教義抽象觀念。

4.1 Second Temple Trajectories: Peshat, Midrash, Pesher, and Philonic Allegory

The dialectic between literal fidelity and spiritual actualization originated in Second Temple Judaism (ca. 516 BCE–70 CE). As Michael Fishbane demonstrates, inner-biblical exegesis already operated via the dynamic tension between received sacred tradition (traditum) and its ongoing contextual reinterpretation (traditio), as when Deutero-Isaiah cast the Babylonian return as a New Exodus.28 By the turn of the era, Jewish interpretation bifurcated into distinct methodological streams. Proto-rabbinic exegesis balanced peshat (plain contextual sense) with midrash (from darash, "to inquire"), branching into halakhah (legal derivation regulated by Hillel's seven middoth, such as Qal vaChomer and Gezerah Shavah, later expanded to thirteen by Rabbi Ishmael) and haggadah (homiletical narrative expansion).29

By contrast, the Qumran covenanters practiced apocalyptic pesher (from pāšar, "to unloose"; e.g., 1QpHab, 4QpNah), reading prophetic lemmas atomistically through the formula "this is that" (pishrô ‘al...) to decode contemporary sectarian history as disclosed to the Teacher of Righteousness.30 Meanwhile, in Diaspora Alexandria, Philo (ca. 20 BCE–50 CE) synthesized the Torah with Middle Platonism and Stoic Homeric defense: the literal text functioned as the mortal body (sōma), while the hidden allegorical meaning (hyponoia) constituted the rational soul (psychē), turning Genesis narratives into interior dramas of the intellect (nous) conquering passions.31

4.2 The Patristic Divide: Alexandrian Allegoria vs. Antiochene Theoria

Early Christian exegesis inherited both Hellenistic allegory and Semitic historical realism, producing a decisive division between Alexandria and Antioch. Building on Clement of Alexandria, Origen (ca. 185–254 CE) articulated in De Principiis (4.2.4) a tripartite sense corresponding to Pauline anthropology (1 Thess 5:23): bodily/literal (sarkikos), psychic/moral (psychikos), and spiritual/allegorical (pneumatikos).32 Origen contended that the Spirit intentionally embedded historical discrepancies as stumbling blocks (skandala) to compel mature readers beyond the letter, as exemplified in his Christological allegorization of the Good Samaritan (Homilies on Luke 34).33

The Antiochene school—led by Diodore of Tarsus, Theodore of Mopsuestia, and John Chrysostom—vigorously resisted this dissolution of historia. They distinguished illicit allegoria from theoria (spiritual vision), insisting that higher prophetic insight must remain anchored in grammatical-historical reality without abolishing its factual substrate.34 As Leonhard Goppelt's classic study Typos demonstrates, genuine biblical typology requires both historical correspondence and redemptive escalation (Steigerung). In the West, Augustine's De Doctrina Christiana mediated this legacy by distinguishing literal signs (signa propria) from figurative signs (signa translata) under the twin controls of the regula fidei and the Law of Double Love.35

4.3 Medieval Quadriga, Thomistic Literalism, and the Reformation Shift

Medieval exegesis systematized patristic multiplicity into the fourfold Quadriga—literal (deeds), allegorical (faith), tropological (moral duty), and anagogical (eschatological hope)—enshrined in the Glossa Ordinaria. Yet in Summa Theologiae (I, q. 1, a. 10), Thomas Aquinas introduced a crucial semiotic constraint: while God can signify spiritual realities through the things signified by words, the sensus literalis remains the sole foundation of theology, and no dogma may be established from spiritual senses alone. Nicholas of Lyra's Postilla litteralis further retrieved Hebrew philology and Rashi's peshat, paving the way for the sixteenth-century Reformation.36

The Reformers executed a decisive discontinuity by repudiating the Quadriga and condemning arbitrary allegorization as a malleable "wax nose" (nasus cereus). Anchored in sola Scriptura (the supreme norma normans, functioning alongside ministerial Tradition I rather than ahistorical "solo scriptura"), Luther's dual clarity (claritas externa and claritas interna), and Scriptura sui ipsius interpres, John Calvin championed the single, natural literal sense (sensus literalis et simplex) within covenantal history while preserving disciplined Christocentric typology. Across this pre-modern arc from Ezra's scribes to Geneva's academy, the fundamental tension was never between "spiritual" and "unspiritual" readers, but between methods that anchored theological meaning inside Israel's concrete covenantal history and methods that dissolved historical particularity into timeless philosophical or dogmatic abstraction.

4.1 第二聖殿軌跡：字面原意、米德拉什、佩舍與斐羅寓意

字面忠誠與屬靈當代化之間的張力，源於第二聖殿時期的猶太教（約主前 516 至主後 70 年）。誠如費什班（Michael Fishbane）所證，正典內部釋經早已透過「受領傳統」（traditum）與「傳承再釋」（traditio）的動態辯證運作——例如第二以賽亞將巴比倫歸回塑造成「新出埃及」。28 降至兩約之間，猶太釋經分化為數條路徑：早期拉比平衡了字面上下文意義（peshat）與探究式釋經（midrash，源自darash「尋求」），後者再分為律法推導的「哈拉卡」（halakhah，受希列七大法則〔middoth〕如Qal vaChomer「舉輕以明重」與Gezerah Shavah「字詞類比」規範，並由拉比以實瑪利擴充為十三條）與敘事講道性的「哈加達」（haggadah）。29

相較之下，昆蘭聖約群體實踐天啟式的pesher（源自pāšar「解開」；見於 1QpHab、4QpNah），透過「這就是那」（pishrô ‘al...）的原子化公式，將先知經文抽離原初語境並解碼為「公義教師」（Teacher of Righteousness）所領受的當代末世歷史。30 與此同時，在離散地亞歷山大城，斐羅（Philo）將摩西五經與中期柏拉圖主義及斯多亞荷馬護教法融合：字面經文如同會朽壞的身體（sōma），隱藏的寓意（hyponoia）則是理性的靈魂（psychē），將創世記敘事轉化為理性（nous）克勝情慾的心靈劇碼。31

4.2 教父時期的分裂：亞歷山大派「寓意」與安提阿派「靈視」

早期教會同時繼承了希臘化寓意法與閃族歷史實在論，進而形成亞歷山大與安提阿兩大學派的著名對峙。承繼革利免的俄利根（Origen）在《論首要原則》（De Principiis 4.2.4）中提出對應保羅三元人論（帖前 5:23）的三重意義：屬肉體／字面義（sarkikos）、屬魂／道德義（psychikos）與屬靈／寓意義（pneumatikos）。32 俄利根主張聖靈故意在字面經文中埋下歷史矛盾作為「絆腳石」（skandala），迫使成熟信徒超越字句，正如其對好撒瑪利亞人比喻的基督論寓意講解（《路加福音講道集》34）。33

由大數的狄奧多爾（Diodore of Tarsus）、摩普綏提亞的狄奧多爾與約翰屈梭多模領銜的安提阿學派，則堅決反對消解historia（歷史敘事）。他們嚴格區分非法的allegoria與theoria（先知性靈視），強調更高的屬靈洞見必須紮根於歷史文法實在之上，絕不可摧毀其事實根基。34 正如戈佩爾特（Leonhard Goppelt）在經典名著《預表》（Typos）中所論證，真正的聖經預表論兼備「歷史對應性」與「救贖升級」（Steigerung）。在西方，奧古斯丁於《論基督教教義》中透過區分字面記號（signa propria）與轉義記號（signa translata），並以regula fidei（信仰準則）與「雙重愛律」加以規範，為教父與中世紀搭起橋樑。35

4.3 中世紀四重意義法、阿奎那的字面優先與宗教改革轉向

中世紀解經將教父遺產系統化為「四重意義法」（Quadriga）——字面義（歷史事件）、寓意義（信仰教義）、道德義（倫理實踐）與末世義（天上盼望），並彙編於《標準註釋》。然而阿奎那（Thomas Aquinas）在《神學大全》（Summa Theologiae I, q. 1, a. 10）中立下了決定性的符號學界線：唯獨上帝能使字詞所指涉的事物本身再指涉其他屬靈實在，但sensus literalis仍是神學的唯一根基，任何教義皆不可單憑屬靈意義建立。十四世紀里拉的尼古拉（Nicholas of Lyra）更藉助希伯來文與拉希（Rashi）註釋重振字面釋經，為宗教改革鋪路。36

十六世紀改教家發動了果斷的典範斷裂：他們廢棄四重意義法，痛斥任意寓意化使聖經淪為任人捏塑的「蠟製鼻子」（nasus cereus）。立基於sola Scriptura（聖經作為最高「規範他者而不受規範之準則」，即范胡澤所謂尊重歷史信經的「第一傳統」，而非反歷史的「單獨聖經主義」）、路德的雙重清晰性（claritas externa 與 claritas interna）以及「以經解經」（Scriptura sui ipsius interpres），加爾文確立了植根於聖約歷史的單一自然字面原意（sensus literalis et simplex），同時保留了受歷史約束的基督論預表。縱觀從以斯拉文士傳統到日內瓦學院的前現代軌跡，根本張力從不在於「屬靈」與「不屬靈」的讀者之間，而在於究竟將神學意義錨定於以色列具體的聖約歷史之內，抑或將歷史特殊性消融於超時間的哲學或教義抽象之中。

05 · ENLIGHTENMENT & MODERN TURNS 05 · 啟蒙斷裂與現代轉向

● EPISTEMIC RUPTURE · SENSUS VS. VERITAS 認識論斷裂 · 歷史意義與神學真理之切割

Enlightenment historicism emancipated philology from ecclesiastical control by severing historical meaning (sensus) from theological truth (veritas), yet its closed naturalistic continuum created an unbridgeable chasm between past artifact and contemporary faith. 啟蒙歷史主義將語文學從教會控制中解放出來，卻同時把歷史意義（sensus）與神學真理（veritas）強行切割；其封閉的自然主義因果網，在古代文物與當代信仰之間劃下了一道鴻溝。

5.1 The Enlightenment Rupture: Spinoza, Semler, Wettstein, and Troeltsch

The seventeenth and eighteenth centuries witnessed the most radical epistemological rupture in biblical historiography: the dethronement of ecclesiastical and canonical authority in favor of autonomous human reason. In his Tractatus Theologico-Politicus (1670), Baruch Spinoza formulated the foundational axiom of modern historical criticism: methodus interpretandi scripturae a methodo interpretandi naturae non differt (the method of interpreting Scripture does not differ from interpreting nature).37 Spinoza severed historical meaning (sensus—what the human author intended) from philosophical or theological truth (veritas). Johann Salomo Semler (1725–1791) subsequently dismantled canonical uniformity by distinguishing historically conditioned Schrift (Scripture) from transcendent moral truth (Wort Gottes), while Johann Jacob Wettstein (Novum Testamentum Graecum, 1751–1752) articulated the "historical difference" separating the ancient Mediterranean horizon from modern dogmatics.38

Gotthold Ephraim Lessing famously named the resulting crisis a "broad, ugly ditch" between accidental truths of history and necessary truths of reason. By 1898, Ernst Troeltsch codified classical historicism into three methodological principles: (1) Criticism (Kritik), rendering all historical judgments merely probable and open to revision; (2) Analogy (Analogie), restricting historical plausibility to events analogous to present common experience; and (3) Correlation (Korrelation), binding all phenomena within an unbroken, immanent causal nexus (Kausalnexus) that excludes divine intervention.39 While source, form, and redaction criticisms yielded unprecedented diachronic precision, Troeltschian immanence stranded the text in an alienated past.

5.2 Romantic, Relational, and Existential Counter-Movements

Enlightenment rationalism provoked a series of counter-turns that relocated meaning from detached antiquarian objects to human subjectivity, relationality, and existential being. Friedrich Schleiermacher (1768–1834) established general hermeneutics as the universal art of understanding across grammatical (linguistic system) and psychological (authorial consciousness) axes, articulating the hermeneutical circle between part and whole; Wilhelm Dilthey (1833–1911) distinguished causal explanation (Erklären) in the natural sciences from empathetic understanding (Verstehen) in the humanities (Geisteswissenschaften).40 Earlier still, Johann Georg Hamann mounted a Counter-Enlightenment critique of J. D. Michaelis's detached philology, contrasting Enlightenment "divorce" (Scheidekunst) with incarnational "coupling" (Ehekunst)—a relational hermeneutic of friendship among author, text, and reader.41

In the twentieth century, Karl Barth's Römerbrief (1919/1922) shattered liberal historicism by confronting readers with the sovereign Word of God across an "infinite qualitative distinction." Concurrently, Rudolf Bultmann fused historical criticism with Heidegger's existential phenomenology, arguing that the New Testament's three-tiered cosmology must undergo Entmythologisierung (demythologization) and Sachkritik to disclose authentic human existence (eigentliche Existenz) through the interpreter's inescapable Vorverständnis.42

5.3 The Linguistic Turn, Ricoeur, and the Durham Comparative Debate

Late twentieth-century hermeneutics pivoted from existential consciousness to language, narrative, and communal practice. Paul Ricoeur mediated explanation and understanding through his hermeneutical arc, arguing that written discourse achieves semantic autonomy and projects a "world in front of the text" (le monde du texte) inviting transformative inhabitation.43 Drawing on Ludwig Wittgenstein's language games (Sprachspiele) and forms of life (Lebensformen) alongside Alasdair MacIntyre's tradition-constituted rationality, contemporary theorists exposed the myth of neutral, tradition-free reason.44

This linguistic turn culminated in the sharp methodological debate documented in John M. G. Barclay and B. G. White's The New Testament in Comparison (2020): whereas C. Kavin Rowe (One True Life) argues that Stoicism and early Christianity are incommensurable traditions whose terms cannot be neutrally translated, Troels Engberg-Pedersen, Barclay (deploying Jonathan Z. Smith's triadic comparison of selection, generalization, and redescription), Simon Gathercole (distinguishing resemblance from genealogical relation), and Margaret M. Mitchell defend disciplined scholarly comparison as an indispensable heuristic lens.45 Together, these twentieth-century turns exposed the impossibility of presuppositionless historical objectivism while demonstrating that linguistic, literary, and comparative rigor need not capitulate to either naturalistic reductionism or arbitrary subjectivism.

5.1 啟蒙斷裂：斯賓諾莎、塞姆勒、韋特施泰因與特勒爾奇

十七至十八世紀見證了聖經詮釋史上最劇烈的認識論斷裂：教會與正典權威遭到廢黜，讓位於自主的人類理性。斯賓諾莎在《神學政治論》（1670）中立下了現代歷史批判的基石公理：methodus interpretandi scripturae a methodo interpretandi naturae non differt（詮釋聖經的方法與詮釋自然的方法並無二致）。37 斯賓諾莎將歷史意義（sensus，即人類作者的意圖）與哲學或神學真理（veritas）徹底切割。隨後，塞姆勒（Johann Salomo Semler）區分受歷史條件制約的「聖經文獻」（Schrift）與超越的道德真理「上帝之道」（Wort Gottes），拆解了正典的一致性；而韋特施泰因（Johann Jacob Wettstein）則在《希臘文新約》（1751–1752）中揭示了隔絕古代地中海視域與現代教義系統的「歷史差異」（historical difference）。38

萊辛（G. E. Lessing）將此危機稱為「偶然的歷史真理」與「必然的理性真理」之間「又寬又醜的鴻溝」。至 1898 年，特勒爾奇（Ernst Troeltsch）將古典歷史主義系統化為三大方法論原則：（一）批判原則（Kritik），宣告一切歷史判斷僅具暫時概然性；（二）類比原則（Analogie），將歷史可能性限縮於與當代常態經驗相似的事件；（三）關聯原則（Korrelation），把一切現象鎖入封閉的內在因果網（Kausalnexus），預先排除了神聖超自然的介入。39 儘管來源、形式與編修批判帶來了前所未有的歷時精確度，特勒爾奇式的內在論卻將聖經文本囚禁在疏離的過去之中。

5.2 浪漫主義、關係性與存在主義的反動

啟蒙理性主義隨即激發了一連串反轉向，將意義的重心從冷冰冰的考古文物重新移回人類的主體性、關係性與存在處境。施萊爾馬赫（Friedrich Schleiermacher）建立「一般詮釋學」，主張在語法（語言系統）與心理（作者意識）雙軸上理解文本，並提出部分與整體之間的「詮釋學循環」；狄爾泰（Wilhelm Dilthey）則區分自然科學的因果「解釋」（Erklären）與人文科學（Geisteswissenschaften）的同情「理解」（Verstehen）。40 更早之前，哈曼（Johann Georg Hamann）已批判米哈埃利斯（J. D. Michaelis）抽離情感的語文學，以道成肉身為根基，用「結合之藝」（Ehekunst）對抗啟蒙的「離異之藝」（Scheidekunst），倡導作者、文本與讀者之間的友誼詮釋學。41

二十世紀初，巴特（Karl Barth）的《羅馬書釋義》以神人之間的「無限質性差異」粉碎了自由派歷史主義，迫使讀者在聖言危機前戰兢。與此同時，布特曼（Rudolf Bultmann）結合歷史批判與海德格的存在現象學，主張新約的三層宇宙觀必須經過「去神話化」（Entmythologisierung）與「實質批判」（Sachkritik），方能透過詮釋者無可迴避的「前理解」（Vorverständnis）揭示真實的人類存在（eigentliche Existenz）。42

5.3 語言學轉向、呂格爾與杜倫比較方法論論戰

二十世紀末的詮釋學從存在意識轉向語言、敘事與群體實踐。呂格爾（Paul Ricoeur）透過「詮釋學弧」（hermeneutical arc）調解了「解釋」與「理解」的對立，論證書寫語篇具有語義自主性，並在「文本之前投射出一個世界」（le monde du texte），邀請讀者進入其中活出新的存在方式。43 結合維根斯坦（Ludwig Wittgenstein）的「語言遊戲」（Sprachspiele）與「生活形式」（Lebensformen），以及麥金泰爾（Alasdair MacIntyre）的「傳統構成之理性」，當代學者徹底揭破了超然中立理性的神話。44

此語言學轉向在巴克萊（John M. G. Barclay）與懷特（B. G. White）合編的《比較中的新約》（2020）所記載的杜倫論戰中達到高峰：羅（C. Kavin Rowe）主張斯多亞主義與早期基督教屬於不可通約的競爭性生活傳統，詞彙無法在傳統之外進行中立翻譯；而英伯格—佩德森、巴克萊（採用史密斯〔Jonathan Z. Smith〕的選取、概括與重述三元比較模型）、蓋瑟科爾（區分「相似性」與「譜系關聯」）以及米契爾（Margaret M. Mitchell）則捍衛受紀律約束的學術比較，視之為不可或缺的啟發式透鏡。45 這幾波二十世紀轉向共同揭破了「無預設歷史客觀主義」的不可能，同時證明語言學、文學與比較方法的嚴謹性，絕無須向自然主義化約論或任意主觀主義妥協。

Canonical Recovery and Contemporary Integrated Exegesis 正典復歸與當代整合釋經之雙向典範

● CONTEMPORARY PARADIGM · BILATERAL SYNTHESIS 當代典範 · 歷史文法與正典神學之雙向整合

Contemporary integrated exegesis transcends the false dichotomy between historical-critical atomism and ungrounded theological reading by anchoring canonical speech-acts and reception history in rigorous original-language philology. 當代整合釋經超越了「歷史批判原子化」與「無根神學解讀」之間的虛假二分法，將正典言語行動與領受史牢牢錨定於嚴謹的原文語文學之中。

6.1 The Canonical Turn: Brevard S. Childs and James A. Sanders

By the 1970s, the fragmentation of Scripture into hypothetical pre-canonical strata (J, E, D, P; Q) had alienated biblical studies from the confessing church. In Biblical Theology in Crisis (1970) and Introduction to the Old Testament as Scripture (1979), Brevard S. Childs inaugurated the Canonical Approach, arguing that the normative locus of theological authority resides not in reconstructed genetic pre-histories, but in the final canonical shape of the text received by the covenant community.46 Whereas James A. Sanders emphasized the dynamic canonical process by which traditions were adapted across Israel's history, Childs insisted that canonization deliberately loosened texts from ephemeral historical origins so they might address all subsequent generations.47 Complementing this canonical horizon, Francis Watson (Paul and the Hermeneutics of Faith) demonstrated how early Christian exegesis operated within both intra-canonical scriptural intertextuality and its wider Jewish and extra-canonical Christian literary environment.48

6.2 Theological Interpretation (TIS): Vanhoozer's Theodrama and Bockmuehl's Ecclesial Horizon

Building on canonical criticism and speech-act philosophy (J. L. Austin, John Searle, Nicholas Wolterstorff), Kevin J. Vanhoozer spearheaded the Theological Interpretation of Scripture (TIS) in Is There a Meaning in This Text? (1998) and The Drama of Doctrine (2005).49 Vanhoozer conceptualizes Scripture as divine communicative action operating across locutionary (verbal/syntactic), illocutionary (promising, commanding, asserting), and perlocutionary (transformative) dimensions: the canon serves as the authoritative script of the theodrama, prompted by the Holy Spirit and performed by the church.50

In parallel, Markus Bockmuehl's Seeing the Word (2006) challenged disinterested historicism by recovering the text's implied reader—an apostolic believer situated within the living memory of the church—and integrating early patristic Wirkungsgeschichte into critical New Testament scholarship.51 Rather than treating the early church fathers and the lives of the saints as irrelevant post-apostolic corruptions, Bockmuehl demonstrates that their readings preserve vital linguistic, liturgical, and theological resonances embedded within the New Testament's own communicative horizon.

6.3 Critical Realism, the Hermeneutical Spiral, and Bilateral Exegesis

Contemporary postgraduate exegesis synthesizes these historical, linguistic, and theological turns under three converging safeguards. First, Grant R. Osborne's hermeneutical spiral replaces the vicious circle of subjectivity with an upward, self-correcting trajectory in which grammatical-historical exegesis continuously tests and refines the interpreter's pre-understandings across biblical, historical, and systematic theology.52 As the interpreter moves from textual criticism and lexical-syntactic analysis to genre competence and canonical theology, the biblical text actively corrects distorted expectations. Second, Craig L. Blomberg and Jennifer Foutz Markley deploy the "ladder of abstraction" (principlizing) to move faithfully from ancient cultural particularity to supracultural theological principles and contemporary ethical application, preventing both flat legalistic transfer and arbitrary subjective allegorization.53

Third, N. T. Wright, Michael F. Bird, and Anthony C. Thiselton ground this integrated method in critical realism: rejecting both naive Enlightenment empiricism and postmodern deconstruction, critical realism affirms that an objective historical past is genuinely accessible through disciplined philological, intertextual (Richard B. Hays), and fallacy-aware (James Barr, D. A. Carson) exegesis, while humbly acknowledging our hermeneutically situated lenses.54 As David J. Downs and Joel B. Green insist, all historical study is invariably theological—and valid theological interpretation never bypasses the literal, grammatical-historical sense. In doctoral-level practice, this bilateral synthesis operates across three sequential yet mutually informing lenses: (1) primary original-language exegesis attentive to textual criticism, verbal aspect, and socio-historical context; (2) literary, discourse, and comparative analysis calibrated by Hays's seven criteria of echo and Jonathan Z. Smith's triadic comparison; and (3) canonical-theological synthesis that reads each pericope within the two-testament drama of redemption and its ecclesial reception history, ensuring that historical precision and confessional fidelity mutually reinforce one another. Taken together, this integrated framework fulfills the highest aspiration of hermeneutical historiography: honoring the historical otherness of the biblical text while allowing its living, Spirit-empowered communicative action to address and reform the contemporary church. In this bilateral posture, philological rigor and theological fidelity cease to be adversaries; grammatical-historical exegesis guards the church against self-serving eisegesis, while canonical and reception-historical awareness rescues grammatical exegesis from antiquarian irrelevance, uniting the academy and the confessing community in attentive listening to the Word of God. Furthermore, this synthesis prepares the postgraduate researcher to engage the seven historical epochs in Part I not as obsolete museum exhibits, but as living conversation partners whose triumphs and errors continue to instruct twenty-first-century interpreters. By recognizing both the historical distance that separates us from ancient Israel and the early church and the canonical continuity that binds us to the same triune God, contemporary exegesis achieves a disciplined fusion of horizons that honors both scholarly integrity and ecclesial faithfulness. Every subsequent chapter of Part I will therefore trace how these twin obligations—philological fidelity to the ancient text and theological responsiveness to the living canon—were negotiated, contested, and renewed across two millennia of Jewish and Christian interpretation.

6.1 正典轉向：柴爾茲與桑德斯

到了二十世紀七〇年代，歷史批判法將聖經支解為假設性的前正典底本（如 J、E、D、P 與 Q 來源），使聖經學術研究與認信教會日益疏離。柴爾茲（Brevard S. Childs）在《危機中的聖經神學》（1970）與《作為聖經的舊約導論》（1979）中開創了「正典進路」（Canonical Approach），論證神學權威的規範性所在並非重構出來的前史碎片，而是聖約群體所領受的「最終正典形態」（final canonical shape）。46 相對於桑德斯（James A. Sanders）側重傳統在以色列歷史中不斷調適的動態「正典化過程」（canonical process），柴爾茲強調正典化刻意將經文從短暫的原初歷史處境中鬆綁，使其能向歷世歷代發言。47 與此相輔相成，華生（Francis Watson）在《保羅與信心的詮釋學》中揭示了早期基督教釋經如何在正典內部互涉與更廣泛的猶太及正典外基督教文學環境之間運作。48

6.2 聖經神學詮釋（TIS）：范胡澤的神學戲劇與博克穆爾的教會視域

承繼正典批判與言語行動哲學（奧斯汀、瑟爾、沃爾特斯托夫），范胡澤（Kevin J. Vanhoozer）在《此文本中有意義嗎？》（1998）與《教義的戲劇》（2005）中奠定了「聖經的神學詮釋」（Theological Interpretation of Scripture, TIS）之理論根基。49 范胡澤將聖經概念化為橫跨「言內行動」（字詞句法）、「言外行動」（應許、命令、斷言）與「言後效應」（生命轉化）的神聖溝通行動：正典乃是「神學戲劇」（theodrama）的權威劇本，由聖靈提詞並由教會忠實演出。50

與此平行，博克穆爾（Markus Bockmuehl）的《看見聖言》（2006）透過重拾文本的「隱含讀者」——即置身教會活潑記憶中的使徒性信徒——並將早期教父的Wirkungsgeschichte納入嚴謹的新約研究，有力地挑戰了抽離信仰的歷史主義。51

6.3 批判實在論、詮釋學螺旋與雙向整合釋經

當代博士級釋經學透過三道匯流的防護機制，整合了上述歷史、語言與神學轉向。首先，奧斯本（Grant R. Osborne）的「詮釋學螺旋」（hermeneutical spiral）以向上自我校正的軌跡取代主觀惡性循環，使歷史文法釋經得以在聖經神學、歷史神學與系統神學之間持續檢驗並修正詮釋者的前理解。52 其次，布隆伯格（Craig L. Blomberg）與馬克利（Jennifer Foutz Markley）採用「抽象階梯」（ladder of abstraction）原則化方法，從古代文化特殊性謹慎提取超文化的神學原則，再下降至當代倫理應用。53

第三，賴特（N. T. Wright）、柏德（Michael F. Bird）與西瑟頓將此整合方法論錨定於「批判實在論」（critical realism）：既拒絕啟蒙時期天真的實證主義，也拒絕後現代解構主義，肯定客觀的歷史過去可透過嚴謹的原文語法、文本互涉（Richard B. Hays）與謬誤控管（James Barr, D. A. Carson）加以認識，同時謙卑承認我們受歷史處境制約的觀察透鏡。54 誠如唐斯（David J. Downs）與格林（Joel B. Green）所強調，一切歷史研究無可避免皆具神學性，而正當的神學詮釋絕不繞過字面的歷史文法意義。在博士級學術實踐中，此雙向綜合透過三個既依序推進又彼此滋養的透鏡運作：（一）專注文本鑑別、動詞體貌與社會歷史處境的第一序原文釋經；（二）以海斯「回響七標準」與史密斯「三元比較模型」校準的文學、語篇與比較分析；以及（三）將每段經文置於雙約救贖戲劇與教會領受史中閱讀的正典神學綜合，使歷史精確性與認信忠誠彼此印證、相輔相成。
